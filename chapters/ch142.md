# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Forty-Two — Axis-7's Intervention

She came back ten days later, and this time she brought someone with her.

Not another independent visit. Not personal investigation. The circuit network showed two structured signatures approaching the parking structure at 11 PM — Lady Shen's familiar 80-meter ambient projection, and behind it a second signature that he didn't have in his records, higher-capability than Lady Shen's but more contained, moving with the specific economy of someone who had been doing this for longer than Lady Shen had been alive.

He was already in the Strata when they crossed the threshold.

He had known this was possible. The Northern Peak Council's session had ended inconclusively ten days ago. Lady Shen had been in his archive, training, showing every sign of genuine engagement. But she was also still a senior operative of the Court, and the Court had a sixty-percent faction that wanted direct access, and the Council's ability to override an independent operative's personal approach had apparently been used.

She was here under instruction now.

He felt the signatures move through the main hall — the floor's illumination tracking their passage, the Keeper station's monitoring registering two simultaneous presences rather than one. He remained at the library table. He did not go to meet them.

They came through the library door together.

The second practitioner was a man in his sixties, with the posture of someone who had been powerful for long enough that power had become simply his resting state. He looked at the archive shelves the way the Northern Peak Council must have wanted Lady Shen to look at them: as an acquisition opportunity.

Lady Shen looked at Lin Mushi. Her Qi field, in the Colony Link's close-range reading, had a quality he hadn't seen from her before — something that was not quite apology and not quite warning but carried elements of both.

She had not chosen this visit.

The senior practitioner said: "You are the archive's Carrier."

"I'm a practitioner with access to the archive," Lin Mushi said. "The Carrier designation is one I use for convenience."

"The Northern Peak Court has authorized me to negotiate access terms on behalf of the Council."

"The archive's access terms are the Formican community's to set," he said. "Not mine."

"Then produce a Formican representative."

He had been about to explain that this required advance arrangement through the Keeper station's communication system, that it wasn't a matter of producing representatives on demand — when the library's ambient Qi field changed.

Not dramatically. Not with a sound or a flash or any of the signals that dramatic things typically employed.

Axis-7 simply appeared at full scale.

He had seen her at Ratio 2.5 — approximately cat-sized, precise and contained. At full scale — at Ratio 1, uncompressed — she was approximately human height, which was not the dramatic fact. The dramatic fact was that at Ratio 1, her complete exoskeleton was visible in a way that compressed scale obscured: the chitin-metal composite running from the base of her structure to the crown, the dark striations along the joint lines carrying the active amber glow of working circuit inscriptions, the compound eyes operating at a full 240-degree field that registered every point in the room simultaneously.

She radiated a Qi field that had no analog in anything he had encountered from a human practitioner.

The senior practitioner took three steps backward.

Lady Shen, to her credit, did not move.

Axis-7 looked at the senior practitioner for four seconds. She did not look at Lin Mushi. This was not for his benefit.

She said, in the communication register — which Lin Mushi heard at 90% fluency and which the two practitioners could not hear at all, but which propagated through the ambient Qi field in a way that meant both of them felt its quality if not its content:

*This space is not available for Northern Peak Court development.*

She then looked at Lin Mushi.

The signal she sent him specifically: *Tell them what I said.*

He translated.

The senior practitioner looked at him. "The entity — communicated?"

"Yes."

"In what language?"

"The Formican communication register. I'll spare you the technical details." He met the man's gaze. "The archive has its own authority structure. That authority has made its position clear. The Northern Peak Court's access terms will be discussed on the archive's schedule, not the Council's."

The senior practitioner looked at Axis-7.

Axis-7 did not look back. She had turned to the east wall and was reading.

He left within four minutes, which was faster than Lin Mushi had expected.

Lady Shen remained.
