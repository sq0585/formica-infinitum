# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Ninety-Three — Month Seven

Seven months after Ren Wuji died at 3:17 AM in the Wuhou 7-Eleven, Lin Mushi sat at his desk on a Tuesday and did the assessment.

He did this every month now. The habit had started as a practical necessity and had become something more like a ritual — not a ceremony, which would have been unlike him, but a deliberate pause before continuing. The act of writing an accurate assessment required him to distinguish between what he actually had and what he'd been telling himself he had, which were occasionally the same thing and occasionally not.

---

> *Month 7 assessment:*
>
> *Compression: stable at 30% spatial presence (Ratio 1.5) for 8 minutes, working toward 12.5% (Ratio 2 extended duration). Directional release: operational, tested in environment (plant, tributary repair attempt). Ratio 1.7 for inscription work: stable.*
>
> *Chitin Circuits: first generation operational, second generation operational, third generation in design. Two units deployed (one with me, one with Chen Yufei). Function: ambient Qi detection and amplification, cycling, 6–12 meter range depending on ambient density. Duration: 31+ hours.*
>
> *Codex progress: Documents 1–5 complete. Document 6: 70%. Documents 7–?: locked. Communication register (Keeper signal frequency): 30% decoded and rising.*
>
> *Strata knowledge: Layer 2 mapped within 200-meter radius of entry point. Library: outer hall accessed, dimensional locks on container not yet opened. Circular room (Keeper meeting space): 2 visits, 3 communication sessions, approximately 40% comprehension average. Living Formican community confirmed in Layers 3–4.*
>
> *Perception range: 80 meters ambient, 25 meters emotional Qi field, 20 meters structural. Colony Link perception: active at 3–8 meters, beginning to develop room-scale awareness.*
>
> *Channels: 15 primary, 19 auxiliary. All in harmonic.*
>
> *Financial: sustainable. Geological consulting income covers rent and expenses. 3 active contracts. Currency of the work: unglamorous and effective.*
>
> *Alliance: Chen Yufei, formal terms established. Iron Meridian Court: aware of my existence, developing position. Northern Peak Court: active acquisition interest, backup (Lady Shen Baoling) arriving this week.*
>
> *Strata diplomatic status: in progress. Keeper communication established. Authority structure understood at 40%. Prior claim being established.*

He read this back.

He thought: seven months ago I was sitting in the back of a convenience store at 3 AM wondering if I was having a delayed stress response. Now I'm writing a diplomatic strategy for managing my relationship with a subterranean civilization while a senior practitioner from a paranormal institution is en route to collect me.

He thought: this seems like a significant change in my situation.

He thought: and yet the work is the same work. Observe. Record. Understand. Decide.

He noted one new development that required specific attention:

> *Colony Link expansion: I am beginning to perceive room-scale emotional field without direct contact. At a gathering of 3+ people, I can sense the aggregate field of the group. This is new in the past week and is developing faster than the individual sensitivity did.*
>
> *What this means practically: rooms with people in them carry information I didn't previously have access to. Meetings, gatherings, group interactions now have an additional data layer.*
>
> *Concern: this is passive. I did not ask to perceive it. The ethical position of a person who involuntarily receives emotional information about others in group settings is not one I've fully worked out.*
>
> *Practical note: the gathering at Xiao Dong's noodle shop last week — I was receiving aggregate field data from thirty people simultaneously for two hours. This explains the unusual tiredness afterward. Large group exposure has a cost I need to factor in.*

He added:

> *The Colony Link is growing toward something. I don't know yet what that something is. It is in the direction of understanding distributed systems at the human-social scale. This is the dimension of the Seed's growth I am most uncertain about.*
>
> *Rule: document, don't suppress. Understand what it is before deciding what to do with it.*

He capped the pen.

He put the kettle on.

He thought about Lady Shen Baoling arriving in Chengdu and what the next few days were going to require from him, and decided that the assessment was complete and the next step was to eat something proper and then go back into the Strata.

He made rice. He ate it properly, at the table, without reading anything.

Then he went back to the Strata.

He was going to spend the next four hours working on the communication register.

He needed to understand more of what the older Keeper had said.
