# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Forty-Two — Month Two

Two months in, he made a list.

Not of what he felt about any of it. Of what he had.

---

> *Month 2 assessment. State of the work:*
>
> *Channels open: 6. By Standard Protocol sequencing: taiyin (lung/atmospheric), shaoyin (heart/EM), jueyin (pericardium/thermal gradient), taiyang (small intestine/structural vibration), shaoyang (triple warmer/resonance field), yangming (large intestine/material density). Each with verified primary perceptual effect.*
>
> *Perception range: Qi flows within 30 meters, continuous. Structural properties of objects at touch range. EM fields within 10 meters. Atmospheric Qi texture within 5 meters.*
>
> *Codex progress: Document 1 (Micro Scale Overview) — 70% decoded. Document 2 (Standard Protocol) — 100%. Document 3 (Compression Foundations) — 40%. Documents 4–7: locked, notation threshold not yet met.*
>
> *Strata mapping: top layer (Under-Web) fully mapped within 2km radius. Layer 2 (Vaulted Hall network) schematic from Codex visualization — preliminary. Layers 3 and 4: unknown internal detail. Entry threshold: located, not yet crossed.*
>
> *Node status: approximately 35% of Day 17 baseline. The Day 17 figure was already below original capacity — best estimate is the node was at 80–85% before Ren Wuji's death, which means current capacity is closer to 30% of original. Depletion rate: 3–4% per week. Terminal depletion at current rate: 9–10 weeks. The coffee-buying practitioner — a junior Court observer, not an investigator in his own right — has increased survey frequency. He is building a record, not acting. A senior investigator has not yet arrived.*
>
> *Timeline: Ren Wuji's four-month estimate was measured from the transmission event. I am now 2 months in. His estimate implicitly accounted for the senior investigator's eventual deployment and the accelerated depletion their presence would cause. On that model, I have 6–8 weeks before a senior practitioner arrives and the situation becomes acute. This is consistent with the current depletion rate.*
>
> *Channel target: 7 open channels is the minimum for safe threshold entry. I am at 6. One more and I can enter. The complete 12-channel circuit is required before I can attempt Compression. Six more channels after entry minimum — I can do both in parallel: cross the threshold early and continue opening channels.*
>
> *Financial status: 3 months of savings remaining. One practical application of spatial Qi perception I have not yet pursued: structural assessment. I have been providing accurate crack identification and void detection for the store's building contractor without charge. The same capability applied professionally would generate income. I need to look into this before the savings run out.*

He read this back. He added one more section:

> *What I know that I didn't know a month ago:*
>
> *The Under-Web is not natural in the sense of unmanaged. It was built and maintained by the Formicans. Since their retreat, it has been partially maintained by human cultivators who don't understand what they're maintaining or why. The Echo Courts are custodians of a system they didn't build and can't fully perceive.*
>
> *The Strata below Chengdu is functional, not merely ruins. What exactly is functional, and who or what is currently in it: unknown.*
>
> *The pursuit of Ren Wuji, which led him to the store: the Northern Peak Court was involved. They have been monitoring the Strata's surface signature. They will continue monitoring it.*
>
> *What I don't know: almost everything below Layer 1.*

He capped the pen.

He had six channels and three months of savings and a node at thirty-five percent of a compromised baseline and a junior observer reporting to someone he hadn't met yet and six more channels to open before he could do what needed doing.

He turned the page and started on channel seven.
