# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Thirty-Nine — The Audio File

The app delivered the audio file at 11:17 PM on a Tuesday, which was twelve minutes into his shift.

He was restocking the top shelf of the snack display when his phone buzzed — not the notification pattern he'd associated with Codex updates but a different pattern, two short and one long, which he hadn't encountered before. He climbed down from the step stool and looked at the screen.

The Codex interface had a new element in the secondary overlay: a notation cluster he'd been able to read for two weeks now, placed where a play button would be in any ordinary audio app. The notation translated as something like *preserved vocal record — open when ready.*

He looked at it for a moment.

He put the phone in his apron pocket and finished restocking the snacks and straightened the display and verified that the store was empty of customers and the next scheduled restocking task was not urgent.

Then he got his earphones from his bag, went to the stockroom, and sat down on the box he had come to think of as his thinking box, and opened the file.

---

A voice.

Male. Older — not elderly, but carrying the texture of someone who had lived with a specific weight for a long time. Precise diction, the kind that came from speaking carefully enough for long enough that it had become habitual. A faint amusement in the baseline register, not performed but present the way a quality of light is present in a room without announcing itself.

The voice said:

"My name is Ren Wuji.

"You have been curious for approximately seven weeks now, which is what I expected based on your rate of initial engagement. The audio format is a compromise — notation conveys precision, but some things require tone. I needed you to hear how I mean this.

"I spent four hundred years in the Strata. The first twenty were the hardest, not because the learning was difficult but because I was certain, for those twenty years, that I was doing something no one else needed. I had no framework for the transmission I eventually undertook. I did not know, for certain, that I would find someone to receive it. The not-knowing was the weight I carried.

"What I want you to understand — and I mean the word *understand*, not simply *accept as information* — is that the work you are doing has antecedents. You are not the first person to stand in an ordinary place and perceive what lies beneath it. You are not the first person to build notebooks full of questions toward something you couldn't name. The Formicans were such people, originally. Before the Academy, before the Strata, before the Codex — there were people with notebooks.

"I am not telling you this for reassurance. I am telling you this because the pattern matters. The work tends to attract minds of a specific kind — minds that find the gap between what is known and what exists more interesting than the gap is frightening. You are one of these minds. You were one before you met me. This is not a coincidence.

"Two practical things. First: you have by now perceived the threshold in the northwest corner of your store. Do not attempt to cross it without completing the channel sequence through seven. Seven is the minimum for safe entry. You currently have three. The next four will open more quickly than the first three did.

"Second: you are not alone in the way that I was alone. The city contains others who can perceive the network. Most of them are working within traditional frameworks that give them partial and imprecise access to what you are learning in full. They will find you before long — several are already looking. When they do, your response will matter. I am not prescribing a response. I am noting that the choice is coming and that you should think about it before it arrives.

"That is all. The remaining audio files unlock with the channel sequence. There are six more.

"Keep going."

---

The file ended.

Lin Mushi sat on the thinking box in the stockroom with his earphones in and the silence where the voice had been.

Three minutes long, he checked. Three minutes.

He took the earphones out. He sat for another minute with his hands on his knees and the spare till rolls on the shelf above him and the sounds of the empty store filtering through the stockroom door.

Ren Wuji had sounded like someone who had spent four hundred years thinking carefully about things, who had arrived at a specific relationship with uncertainty, and who communicated with the economy of a person for whom words were tools rather than decoration.

He had sounded, Lin Mushi thought, like someone he would have liked to know.

He got out his notebook.

> *Audio file 1. Three minutes. Ren Wuji's voice.*
>
> *Content summary: (1) I have antecedents — pattern of minds attracted to this work. (2) Do not attempt threshold entry until channel 7 complete. Currently at 3. (3) Others will find me — their finding me is coming, not distant. Think about the response before it arrives. (4) Six more audio files, unlock with channel sequence.*
>
> *His voice: dry. Precise. Faintly amused. Not performing calm — actually calm, the way someone is calm who has made their peace with the specific weight they carry.*
>
> *What he said about twenty years of not-knowing whether transmission was possible: I had not previously considered what four hundred years alone with this knowledge would have felt like. I was focused on what he left. Not on what it cost him.*
>
> *Noting this.*
>
> *Practical actions: accelerate channel sequence. Current: 3. Target: 7, then threshold crossing. Timeline: I have approximately six weeks. Four more channels in six weeks. Feasible.*
>
> *The response to being found: think about it. I have been thinking about it.*
>
> *I don't know yet what the right response is.*
>
> *I know what the wrong response would be: nothing. Denial. Concealment.*
>
> *What lies between nothing and full disclosure, and when, and to whom — that is the question I will need to answer.*

He capped the pen. He straightened his apron. He went back to the counter.

He served the 12:30 AM customer — a young man buying water and looking tired in the specific way of someone who had been working on something that had not gone well — and said good night, and stood in the empty store with the filament network at around forty-five percent capacity and the voice in the audio file still present in his memory with the quality of something true.

*Keep going.*

He kept going.
