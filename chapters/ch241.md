# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Two Hundred and Forty-One — Chitin Circuit, Generation Four

The fourth-generation Circuit started as a question he'd been turning over since the early days of the maintenance rotation: how much of what he could do required him specifically, and how much was simply a technique that happened to currently require a cultivator to execute it.

The third-generation Circuits had already encoded basic environmental sensing — Qi density readings, anomaly detection, the kind of passive monitoring that had let him catch Stone Hall's surveillance within minutes. That work had always felt, to him, like translation rather than invention: taking something his own perception did naturally and finding a way to let inert material approximate it.

Dimensional Weaving was a different category of problem entirely. It wasn't perception. It was construction — active, deliberate manipulation of space that, as far as he understood the Codex's framework, required a practitioner's interface to the Veritonal substrate, the same interface Compression trained and refined.

He spent three weeks on the notation before he believed the answer he kept arriving at.

The interface wasn't the only thing capable of producing the geometry. It was simply the most flexible, general-purpose tool currently available for producing it. A sufficiently precise physical structure — chitin matrix, properly inscribed, properly calibrated — could hold a single, narrow, pre-defined fold without needing a practitioner's ongoing attention at all. Not arbitrary Weaving. Not anything close to what he could do freehand. But one specific, fixed pocket, built once and then simply maintained by the structure's own inscribed geometry.

He built the test device on a Sunday, working in the pocket dimension laboratory with the careful, methodical patience the work demanded.

A box, ten centimeters on each side, chitin matrix inscribed with notation he'd refined across eleven failed attempts, powered by nothing but ambient Qi density — no battery, no active practitioner input once activated.

He activated it at 2 PM.

The box's interior held a stable ten-centimeter pocket dimension, indefinitely, without any further input from him at all.

He sat with it for three hours, testing it from every angle he could think of — disrupting the ambient Qi around it, moving it, opening and closing the small inscribed access point repeatedly. The fold held through all of it, stable, self-sustaining, completely indifferent to whether he was paying attention to it or not.

He wrote in his notebook, the weight of what he'd just built settling slowly.

> *Ten-centimeter stable pocket dimension. No Qi input required beyond ambient density. No practitioner attention required after activation. This is the most technically impressive thing I have ever built, and I am not certain yet whether that's a good thing.*
>
> *The implication, if I'm reading this correctly: Dimensional Weaving doesn't have to remain something only a trained practitioner can do. It can be encoded, fixed, made available to anyone who has the device, regardless of their own cultivation level.*
>
> *I need to think carefully about what that actually means before I tell anyone what I've built.*

He closed the notebook and looked at the small box sitting innocuously on the workbench, holding open a fold no larger than a shoebox's corner, and understood, with the particular clarity that arrived whenever a discovery's full weight finally landed, that he had just built something considerably more significant than a clever piece of engineering.

He had built proof that the Courts' entire model of restricted access — knowledge gated behind years of disciplined practice, power concentrated in whoever had the patience and the teacher to acquire it — was not, in fact, the only way this capability could exist in the world.

He sat with the box for a long while before he decided who he needed to show it to first.
