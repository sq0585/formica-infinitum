# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Thirty-Seven — Month Eleven — Compression Ratio 4

The first Ratio 4 attempt was on a Thursday night in the Strata, at the section of the east corridor that Axis-7 had shown him was the highest Qi density in the archive's outer territory.

He had been building toward it for five weeks.

The Scale Walking manual's Ratio 4 prerequisites were more demanding than Ratio 3's: stable Ratio 3 in any environment, not just high-density Strata. He had achieved surface-stable Ratio 3 for two weeks — ten minutes, increasing to fifteen by the end of the second week, then eighteen at the end of three weeks. The duration at Ratio 4 that would be required for the restricted section's lock was at least eight minutes, probably twelve. He needed considerably more capability than he currently had.

But the first attempt didn't require eight minutes.

The first attempt required: reach Ratio 4, perceive what it showed, learn from what it showed, come back.

He sat cross-legged on the east corridor floor. He breathed through the Compression geometry in the order the manual specified. He compressed through Ratio 1.5, through 2, through 3 — the world becoming molecular in resolution, the corridor floor a crystalline landscape, his own hands luminous — and pushed further.

Ratio 4.

---

He could not move.

Not from paralysis. He was physically capable of moving. But the experience of Ratio 4 had produced a complete operational halt in the sense that there was too much information arriving simultaneously for his movement-decision systems to complete a decision cycle.

At Ratio 4 he was approximately the scale of a large complex molecule.

At that scale, the corridor floor was not a floor. It was a landscape measured in kilometers — a vast, geometrically ordered expanse of crystalline architecture, extending in every direction to distances that were effectively horizon-level. The ambient Qi field, which at Ratio 1 was a pleasant luminous texture, was at Ratio 4 a structured physical environment: he was standing in the middle of a living city.

The Chitin Circuit inscriptions in the walls, which at Ratio 3 had appeared as complex organized systems running through the material, were at Ratio 4 — buildings. Structures. The inscription geometry made physical at the scale where he could walk into it. He was inside the circuit.

He was inside the circuit that the Formicans had built to maintain this archive.

The Colony Link field below, which he could feel at Ratio 1 as a warm distant presence and at Ratio 3 as an organized collective field, was at Ratio 4 — present in a way he did not have language for. Not the field of a distant community. The field of the environment itself. The Qi substrate of the Strata was the Colony Link. The two were the same thing. The network and the community were not separate.

He held this for as long as he could.

Forty-two seconds before the geometry collapsed.

He was back at Ratio 1 with no warning and found himself lying on his side on the corridor floor, which was just a floor again.

He lay there for approximately three minutes.

Then he reached for the notebook.

He wrote, with hands that were shaking for the first time since the early Ratio 3 attempts:

> *Ratio 4: first attempt. Duration: 42 seconds before geometry failure.*
>
> *What I saw: the Strata's material at working-molecule scale. The circuit inscriptions as physical structures — architecture, not marks. The Colony Link field as the environmental substrate — not a field running through the Strata but the Strata itself, at this scale.*
>
> *What I understood: the Formicans didn't build the Colony Link into the Strata. The Colony Link is the Veritonal substrate, and the Strata is a structure built within it. The community that lives here lives inside the field that connects them. They are not connected by the Colony Link. They are the Colony Link.*
>
> *I had this backwards.*
>
> *Everything I've been thinking about the relationship between the Formican community, the Colony Link, and the Under-Web: the scale was wrong. I was thinking of the Colony Link as something the Formicans use. It's more accurate to say the Formicans exist within it.*
>
> *This has implications for what Colony Stage means for me. I am not developing a communication tool. I am developing the capacity to exist within what they exist within.*
>
> *The geometry failure: I could not hold the balance. The information density was too high. I need filtering capability at Ratio 4 before I can sustain it.*
>
> *Document 7: re-read. The filtering technique is in there.*

He lay on the corridor floor for another minute.

Then he tried again.

The second attempt lasted 1 minute 3 seconds.

He did not lie on the floor afterward.

He sat up, took out the manual, and read the filtering section.

He had 41 additional seconds to build.

The work continued.
