# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Eleven — Ratio 2 — Molecular Sight

He achieved fifteen minutes on a Monday.

Not in one attempt. He had been building toward it across three weeks, adding thirty seconds per session when he held the previous benchmark cleanly, not pushing when the geometry felt strained. The Scale Walking manual was explicit about this: forcing duration at Ratio 2 created instability patterns in the interface architecture that could persist for days. The correct approach was to extend duration only when the current benchmark felt easy.

Fifteen minutes felt easy on the second attempt on Monday afternoon.

He held it. He came out at fifteen minutes and thirty-seven seconds, deliberately, with a controlled exit that produced no involuntary snap-back.

He opened the next prerequisite threshold on the manual's checklist: *achieve and maintain dual-scale awareness as natural operating mode.*

He had been developing this since the dimensional lock — the ability to hold Ratio-2 geometric awareness while operating at Ratio-1 in the physical environment. He had it. It wasn't effortless yet, but it was natural, the way driving became natural after sufficient practice: still requiring attention, but not requiring concentration.

Both prerequisites met.

He was ready to attempt Ratio 3.

---

He attempted it that evening, in the Strata.

The manual specified: *first Ratio 3 attempts should be made in environments with well-established Veritonal density. The compressed interface at Ratio 3 requires ambient Qi support to maintain. Low-Qi environments significantly increase the difficulty of the first transition.*

The Strata's Qi density was three times the surface ambient level. He entered at 10 PM, told the Keeper station's recognition system he was there by the floor's usual lag-illumination, and walked to a specific section of the outer corridor that he'd identified as having the highest Qi density in the area he'd mapped.

He sat down, because the manual also specified: *perform first Ratio 3 attempts seated. The perceptual shift is significant enough that spatial disorientation is likely. Seated position reduces the re-expansion complexity if the attempt fails.*

He held his current Ratio 2 geometry. Stable, clean, the fourteen channels plus auxiliaries all in harmonic.

He compressed further.

---

He vomited.

Not dramatically — he managed to turn away from the archive materials before it happened — but it happened, and the manual had mentioned it was possible, and he had brought water precisely because the manual had mentioned it was possible.

He sat with his back against the wall of the Strata corridor and breathed carefully and looked at what his perception was now showing him.

Everything was vast.

The floor of the corridor, which at Ratio 1 was hexagonal tiles in a smooth composite material, was at Ratio 3 a landscape. A specific, geometrically extraordinary landscape — the hexagonal geometry of the tiles repeating at the cellular and molecular scales, the active circuit inscriptions running through the material visible as complex organized systems of improbable beauty, the individual molecules of the chitin-composite arrayed in crystalline formation that explained, now that he could see it directly, why the material was capable of sustaining active Qi channels across very long periods without degradation.

His own hands, resting in his lap, were not hands. They were the most complex systems in his current field of view — protein channels visible as luminous structures running through the architecture of his biology, the Chitin Seed's integration extending through the nerve tissue in a crystalline formation that mirrored the Strata's wall material in a way he had suspected theoretically and was now seeing directly.

He was built of the same geometry as the thing he was inside.

He wrote, with hands that were shaking slightly, in the notebook he'd had the presence of mind to keep open on his knee:

> *Ratio 3: achieved. First attempt. Duration: 40 seconds before voluntary exit.*
>
> *Immediate physical effect: nausea (expected), spatial disorientation (expected), significant perceptual overload (expected, managing).*
>
> *What I see at Ratio 3: the Strata's material at cellular-molecular level. The chitin-composite's crystalline structure. The circuit inscriptions running through the material as active organized systems. My own biology at cellular level — protein channels, Chitin Seed integration, nerve tissue architecture.*
>
> *Key observation: the Strata's wall material and my own Chitin Seed integration are structurally similar. The same hexagonal geometry at the same scales. The Strata's material and the Chitin Seed are related technologies.*
>
> *This was obvious in retrospect. The Formicans built both.*

He drank some water. He sat quietly until the nausea passed.

Then he attempted Ratio 3 again, because the manual had said the nausea was acute only on the first few transitions and became manageable with repetition, and he believed the manual.

The second attempt: 1 minute 20 seconds.

He did not vomit.

He did spend three minutes after the exit sitting on the Strata floor staring at the ceiling with the expression of someone who has just looked at something they expected to be large and found it was considerably larger.

The ceiling, at Ratio 3, had been extraordinary.

He went home at midnight and slept for ten hours, which was the most he'd slept since the first Strata entry.

He noted in the morning: *consider this progress.*
