# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Sixty-Three — The Release

The document went to all five Courts simultaneously at 6 AM.

Not curated this time. Not forty-seven names selected for honesty and capacity to engage. He and Chen Yufei sent it to every formal communication channel they could identify — the Iron Meridian's Council secretariat, the Northern Peak's administrative office, the Celestial Compass School's public scholarly register, the Verdant Stream Collective's loose network of regional contacts, and, after some debate about whether the Void Mirror Sect even had a channel that received outside communication, a physical copy delivered by a courier Chen Yufei trusted to an address that may or may not have still been occupied.

He pressed send on the digital portions at 6:00 AM exactly. Chen Yufei confirmed the courier had left ten minutes later.

There was no walking this back.

He sat at his desk afterward with the specific stillness of someone who has just done something that cannot be undone, turning the stillness over to see what was underneath it.

What was underneath it: calm. Not relief, not anxiety. The simple settled quality of having acted in accordance with what he'd determined was true and necessary, regardless of what came next.

He thought, sitting there, about the distance between this morning and the morning fourteen months earlier when he'd sent the first, careful, curated document to forty-seven selected names — the deliberate narrowness of that release, built specifically to avoid exactly the kind of uncontrolled propagation this second document was now guaranteed to produce. He had spent weeks with Chen Yufei reasoning through every argument for narrowing this release the same way. Smaller list. Staged timing. A pilot group of institutional leaders rather than full simultaneous distribution.

He had decided against all of it, in the end, for a reason he wrote down now, while the decision was still fresh enough to articulate honestly rather than retrospectively:

> *Ch.163. The document is out. Full distribution, all five Courts, no curation.*
>
> *Chen Yufei said this would be a catastrophe. I said: or a correction. We don't get to know yet which word turns out to be more accurate. Possibly both will be, at different times, to different people.*
>
> *Why full distribution, this time, rather than the careful narrowing that worked the first time: because the first document was an invitation to ask questions, and invitations can be selective without being dishonest. This document corrects the historical record about technique derivation across all five traditions at once. Selectively informing some institutions and not others about a correction this consequential would have created exactly the kind of asymmetric advantage — some Courts knowing, others not — that the entire project has been trying to dismantle. A correction given unevenly isn't a correction. It's a new kind of leverage.*
>
> *What I know: the information is true. What happens to true information once it leaves your hands is not something you control. You can only control whether you released it honestly, with the corrections attached, structured to help rather than simply to wound, and whether you released it to everyone who needed it rather than only to the people positioned to benefit most from controlling it first.*
>
> *I built it that way. Now it's not mine anymore.*

He closed the notebook.

He went to the Strata, because the work there continued regardless of what was happening on the surface, and because staying in his apartment refreshing his phone for reactions seemed like exactly the wrong way to spend the next several hours.

He spent the morning with the Dimensional Lock practice and the annotation exchange with Meen-4, who made no comment on his evident distraction beyond a single, characteristically dry observation that his notation accuracy had dropped measurably from his usual standard that morning, and that she hoped whatever he had done on the surface was worth the decline.

He told her, briefly, what he had done.

She considered this for a long moment.

*The Layer 3 community will be aware of this within the day,* she sent. *They monitor surface developments connected to the Archive's history more closely than you may realize.*

*Is that a concern?*

*It is information,* she sent, in the flat register he'd learned not to mistake for indifference. *Whether it becomes a concern depends on what the surface does with what you have given it. The same as everything else.*

He found this, oddly, steadying.

His phone, left at the surface entrance per his own protocol, accumulated whatever it accumulated without him.

He found out what that was when he resurfaced at 2 PM.
