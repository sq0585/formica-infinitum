# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter 527 — What Changes

He spent the following week doing something close to an inventory, the same careful, methodical accounting he had performed at every prior threshold, though this one required considerably more patience than most, given the sheer range of what there now was to account for.

Dimensional pockets, he found, could be folded now directly into the Void Lattice's own substrate rather than merely anchored within ordinary space — a distinction that had taken him several days of careful testing to fully understand, and that amounted, in practice, to the difference between building a room inside a house and building a room that was, in some sense, part of the foundation the house itself stood on. Perception extended across the whole stellar neighborhood without effort, the way sight had once extended across a room. And theoretical local spacetime restructuring — a capability the Archive's language had described so carefully, so cautiously, that he had read the relevant passage four times before trusting he'd understood it correctly — sat available to him now, real, tested, confirmed.

He used none of it for anything the word dramatic would have comfortably described.

The eastern residential district of the Strata had carried a persistent drainage problem for the better part of a year — nothing catastrophic, nothing that had ever risen to the level of an actual crisis, simply the low, grinding inconvenience of standing water after heavy rain, three streets that flooded ankle-deep every time Chengdu's summer weather turned serious, a complaint that had sat, patiently, near the bottom of Wei Canglan's very long list of infrastructure priorities for longer than either of them liked to admit.

He fixed it on a Tuesday afternoon.

Not through anything that would have looked, to an observer, like the exercise of power he now genuinely held. He simply extended his attention to the district's underlying geology, felt the specific shape of the drainage problem the way he might once have felt a single Qi channel's minor blockage, and adjusted the substrate's fold at a scale small enough that the change registered, to every sensor and every resident, as nothing more remarkable than a slightly better-graded street.

Wei Canglan noticed within the hour.

*You fixed the eastern drainage,* he sent, through the Anchor Protocol, the message carrying a specific quality Lin Mushi had learned, over years, to recognize as Wei Canglan trying and failing to sound unimpressed.

*I did.*

*You now hold theoretical local spacetime restructuring capability,* Wei Canglan sent, *and your first documented use of it was correcting a drainage grade three streets long.*

*Yes.*

There was a pause, the specific quality of a man recalibrating an expectation he had not realized he was carrying.

*Good,* Wei Canglan sent finally, and Lin Mushi felt, underneath the single word, something that was, for him, close to genuine approval. *That is, I think, exactly the correct use of it. I would have been considerably more concerned by a more ambitious first application.*

*Observation 527,* Lin Mushi wrote that evening, sitting with the day's small, unremarkable accomplishment. *I now hold capability the Archive's own language treats with something close to reverence — theoretical restructuring of local spacetime, dimensional pockets folded directly into the Void Lattice's substrate. My first genuine use of any of it was fixing a drainage problem three residents had been quietly annoyed about for a year. I do not think this represents a failure of ambition. I think it represents, finally, having internalized the entire lesson the last five years were built to teach: the measure of power was never how large a thing it could accomplish. It was always how small a thing it was still willing to bother with.*

He closed the notebook, satisfied, and went to check whether the streets had actually stayed dry.

They had.

He stood at the edge of the eastern residential district a while, watching an entirely ordinary Tuesday evening continue around him — a child chasing a ball across pavement that would have, a week ago, been ankle-deep in standing water; an elderly resident setting out a folding chair in a spot that had, until today, been too damp to sit in comfortably after any real rain; the specific, unremarkable satisfaction of a small problem simply no longer existing.

No one thanked him. No one, as far as he could tell, had any idea the drainage had been fixed by anything more remarkable than a maintenance crew finally getting around to a long-neglected repair. He found, standing there, that he preferred it this way — that the entire measure of whether the capability meant anything real was not how impressed anyone was by its use, but whether the streets, quietly, unremarkably, stayed dry.

He thought of Ren Wuji, briefly, the way he still did most days, in small unbidden moments rather than deliberate remembrance. He thought of a man who had spent thirty years inside a Formican archive building frameworks meant to be transmissible to a student he would never meet, who had died in a convenience store at 3:17 in the morning having given away, deliberately, quietly, everything he had left to give. He understood, standing at the edge of a dry street in a city that had no idea what had almost flooded it, that this was probably closer to what Ren Wuji had actually hoped for than any of the larger, more visible thresholds the last five years had crossed.

He walked home in the ordinary evening light, unremarkable, entirely himself, and found he did not want the evening to be anything other than exactly what it was.
