# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Seventy-Six — Compression — Stable

He had achieved stable Compression — Ratio 1.5, ninety seconds, fully controlled exit — three days before his ninth Strata entry.

He had noted this as a milestone and moved past it. What the stable Compression gave him was not the dramatic transformation he'd been vaguely expecting from the Codex's descriptions of Scale Walking capability. It gave him something more useful: consistency.

Previously, entering the Strata had required active concentration on the Compression geometry throughout his time inside — holding the entry compression the way you'd hold an awkward physical position, aware of it constantly, managing it. Stable Compression meant the geometry maintained itself at the threshold level without continuous attention. He could be inside the Strata and think about other things.

That difference was everything. He had known it intellectually for a week. He understood it fully only on the ninth entry — the Saturday night he walked north and encountered the Keeper for the first time.

---

When the Formican presence took two steps toward him and stopped, Lin Mushi was able to keep writing.

Not because he was calm — he was not entirely calm — but because the Compression was stable and didn't require his attention, which freed his attention for the notebook, which freed his nervous system from having to choose between *maintain the entry conditions* and *observe what is happening in front of you.*

He kept writing. He did not look up.

The presence stopped.

He wrote: *presence has approached to approximately 15 meters. Stopped. No vocalisation, no motion since stopping.*

He looked up.

The Formican was looking at him.

It was — he tried for the accurate descriptor and arrived at *attentive.* Not hostile, not welcoming, not evaluating in the way a human face showed evaluation. Attentive, the way a person looks when they're paying careful attention to what something is doing rather than deciding what to do about it.

He looked back.

He said, in Mandarin: "I'm a practitioner. I inherited a Chitin Seed from someone named Ren Wuji. I've been learning the Codex for five months. I'm sorry about the node."

A silence.

The Formican's Qi field shifted slightly — a change in the field quality that he read, with his current sensitivity, as something between *acknowledgment* and *amusement.* Not laughing — the equivalent of a slight relaxation.

Then it turned and walked north.

It paused and looked back.

---

He followed.

The Codex's Strata navigation sections — which he'd been able to read since Document 5 unlocked — said: *if a Keeper invites passage, follow. If a Keeper does not invite passage, do not follow. This distinction is not subtle.*

It was not subtle.

He followed.

They walked north for another forty meters, to a section of the hall where the columns gave way to an open area and the vault rose higher — perhaps forty-five meters, a ceiling that required Ratio 2 and neck-craning to appreciate properly. In the center of the open area, against the north wall, was a structure he had not been able to see from his earlier positions: a door.

Not a threshold — a door. Actual constructed elements, a frame and a panel, built from the same hexagonal-composite material as the hall, with a notation sequence around the frame that he could now read.

He read it.

*Amber Lattice Library — Main Entrance. Access requires Chitin Seed integration at Micro Scale Stable.*

He had Chitin Seed integration. He had Micro Scale Stable. He had Compression stable at 1.5 and working toward 2.

The door was not locked to him.

The Formican — he was going to need a name for it, or to find out if it had one — stood beside the door and waited.

He walked to the door.

He pushed it open.

The library was inside.

He had arrived at the place the Codex had been leading him to.

He stood in the doorway and felt, for the first time in five months, that he was exactly where he was supposed to be.
