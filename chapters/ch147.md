# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Forty-Seven — What the Northern Peak Knows

Lady Shen's revised intelligence report reached the Northern Peak Council three days after Chen Yufei's expulsion.

He knew this because Elder Kuo sent him a message — not the usual channel through Chen Yufei, but his direct number — that said: *Lady Shen has filed her updated assessment. The Council meets tomorrow. I thought you should know.*

He called Kuo back.

"What does the assessment say?"

"She confirmed Chitin Seed integration, the Strata's existence and active occupation status, and the presence of what she characterized as 'established Formican diplomatic communication protocols.'" A pause. "She recommended against any further direct acquisition or access attempts. Her formal recommendation: escalate from acquisition to political engagement."

He thought about this.

Lady Shen had spent two weeks in the archive working with the training materials. She had watched Axis-7's intervention. She had sat in the library with him and Chen Yufei and read Formican cultivation history and asked questions that got increasingly precise as her comprehension developed. And then she had gone back to her Council and filed a report that said: *stop trying to take this. Start trying to understand it.*

"The Council's likely response?" he asked.

"The sixty percent will resist the recommendation. The forty percent will advocate for it." A pause. "There's a new complication. The Celestial Compass School scholar who independently verified the document — she's been in direct communication with three of your forty-seven recipients and with Lady Shen separately. The Celestial Compass School is not part of the sixty percent's coordination effort. They appear to be building their own relationship with the information."

"Yes. She contacted me two weeks ago."

"You're corresponding with her."

"She has questions about the notation. I've been answering them." He paused. "She has sixty years of astronomical Qi mapping data that she's been trying to reconcile with standard cultivation theory. The Codex's cosmological sections explain most of the discrepancies."

A long silence.

"You've been building a network," Kuo said. Not accusatory. Understanding.

"I've been answering questions from people who are asking genuine questions," he said. "That network is what it is. I didn't plan the topology."

Kuo was quiet.

"The forty percent," Lin Mushi said. "What do they need to prevail in the Council meeting?"

"Evidence that political engagement is more productive than acquisition." A pause. "They have Lady Shen's assessment. They have the document. What they don't have is a concrete example of what productive engagement looks like."

He thought about this.

"Elder Zhao," he said.

"Yes."

"She's been meeting with me informally for three months. She's seen the circuit readings on the Iron Meridian array. She understands the corrected technique at a level that would be meaningful to the Council."

"She's not Northern Peak."

"No. But if you're building the case for political engagement, a practitioner from a different Court who has been engaging constructively and who can speak to what engagement produces — that's more credible than your own Court's recommendations."

Kuo was quiet again.

"I'll ask Elder Zhao," Lin Mushi said. "Whether she'd be willing to speak to what our working relationship has produced. Not on my behalf. On the principle."

"You'd ask her that."

"I'd ask. She can say no."

He called Elder Zhao at noon.

She said she'd think about it, which in Elder Zhao's vocabulary meant she was already drafting what she would say.

He called Kuo back and told him.

The Council meeting was tomorrow. He had done what he could.

He went to the archive for his afternoon session and spent three hours on the Dimensional Lock navigation technique, not thinking about the Council, because thinking about the Council wasn't work he could do and the lock technique was.

The technique improved.

He noted this.

*When there are things you can't act on, work on the things you can. The progress transfers.*
