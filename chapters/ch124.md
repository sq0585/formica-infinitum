# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Twenty-Four — Ratio 3 — Stable

He achieved stable Ratio 3 on a Friday morning in the Strata.

Not in his apartment, not in a controlled surface environment: the manual had been specific about this, and his own instinct confirmed it. Ratio 3 required ambient Qi density that the surface couldn't provide at his current capability level. In the Strata, surrounded by the dense organized field of the archive's maintenance circuits and the Formican community's ambient Colony Link below, the compressed geometry could maintain itself. On the surface it would require effort he didn't yet have.

This was useful information. It meant he currently had a capability that was only accessible underground.

He noted this. He kept working.

Stable Ratio 3 at twenty minutes: he sat cross-legged on the corridor floor sixty meters north of the library door, at the location he'd mapped as the highest Qi density in his regular territory, and held the geometry for twenty-one minutes before deliberately exiting.

No nausea at exit — he'd resolved that in the second week of Ratio 3 training by developing an exit protocol that matched the compression's resolution to the exit's geometry instead of simply releasing. The earlier exits had been rough because he'd been expanding outward randomly. Now he expanded along specific geometric axes, the re-expansion following the compression's internal structure in reverse. Clean.

What he could do at Ratio 3 that he couldn't at Ratio 2:

He could move through the Strata's chitin-composite walls.

Not by destroying them, not by finding gaps — by compressing his interface geometry to a size at which the probability distribution of the material's molecular structure had sufficient space for him to navigate. He had tested this at the library doorframe, pressing his compressed hand into the frame material and finding that at Ratio 3 the material was not solid.

He had not moved his full body through a wall yet. He was building toward that. The manual recommended working up from hands to shoulders to full passage, verifying at each stage that the technique's geometric stability held under the different load.

He wrote:

> *Stable Ratio 3: achieved, month nine.*
>
> *Prerequisites: Ratio 2 stable at 15 minutes (met), dual-scale awareness as natural mode (met), comprehension of matter-as-probability-distribution at relevant scale (met after physics self-education intensive).*
>
> *Capability at Ratio 3: molecular-level perception (confirmed from Ratio 2), passage through non-dense matter (testing in stages), direct observation of Strata material's active circuit inscriptions at working resolution.*
>
> *Current limitation: only stable in high-Qi-density environments (Strata required). Surface stable Ratio 3 requires further development. Timeline: unknown.*
>
> *Ratio 3 limitation that matters for the current situation: Lady Shen Baoling. Her Compression level is approximately 1.3–1.4. She can enter the Strata's outer layers. She cannot do Ratio 3. This creates an access differential inside the Strata — areas that require Ratio 2+ are not accessible to her.*
>
> *The archive's inner areas require Ratio 1.8 for dimensional lock operation. She may not have this.*

He thought about what this meant for the next week.

Lady Shen would enter the Strata. She would see the outer hall, the library's outer section. She would not, by his assessment, be able to access the dimensional locks, the communication register, or the deeper archive areas. She would see the Strata's existence and its general character, but not its content.

She would see enough to understand what it was.

She would not see enough to use it.

This was a reasonable situation, actually.

He wrote:

> *The Strata's access system protects itself automatically. The compression thresholds are not arbitrary security — they're comprehension thresholds. You can only access material you have developed enough to understand.*
>
> *Lady Shen will enter a space she cannot fully access. The question is what she does with that.*
>
> *If she does what Chen Yufei did: asks questions. Accepts information. Begins rebuilding.*
>
> *If she does something else: the situation becomes more complicated.*

He sat in the Strata corridor at Ratio 1 with his notebook and thought about the shape of the next week.

He had a translation document ready. He had forty-seven recipients selected. He had a Strata entry point where Lady Shen would cross within days. He had Axis-7's warning about the network ping's possible consequences arriving from an unknown distance at an unknown time.

It was, he noted, a great deal of simultaneous pressure.

He thought about what Ren Wuji had said in the audio files: *I hope you are curious. Everything depends on it.*

He was curious. He was also, right now, acutely aware that curiosity was one of the things that had created the current situation and was going to have to be the thing that navigated through it.

He stood up.

He walked back to the library.

He went to work.
