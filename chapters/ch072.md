# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Seventy-Two — The Iron Meridian Cultivator

The coffee-buying practitioner came back on a Thursday.

This was his first visit since the node collapsed — Lin Mushi had been tracking the absence and had noted it as significant, consistent with the hypothesis that the man's monitoring function had been reporting to someone who had decided a field change in protocol was warranted. An empty-handed regular returning after a gap was usually someone who'd been given new instructions.

Lin Mushi was not working the night shift anymore. He tracked the man through the ambient Qi field as he passed within sixty meters of his apartment at 2:09 AM — a signature he'd mapped across twenty visits, identifiable now the way a specific voice is identifiable through a wall.

The man was not going to the store. He was walking a wider circuit. A street-level scan, broader range, covering the block where Lin Mushi lived.

He had new instructions. The instructions involved the address from the background inquiry.

---

Lin Mushi sat at his desk and watched through the Qi field's ambient information as the man walked the circuit once, twice, and then departed north.

He did not panic. He had been preparing for this.

He had also, in the past two days, developed a capability he hadn't had before: the new auxiliary channels included one he'd been calling the *directional suppression* channel — which the Codex called something more precise that translated as *scale-selective presence reduction*. It was not invisibility. It was the ability to reduce the ambient Qi his presence broadcast at normal operating range, making his signature smaller and quieter without reducing his perceptual capability.

He practiced it for forty minutes after the man's circuit, reducing his broadcast until the ambient presence he presented to an external scan was roughly equivalent to a non-practitioner — just the baseline Qi signature of a person in a room, without the additional structured output of twelve operating primary channels.

With this active, a practitioner scanning the block at the coffee-buyer's apparent sensitivity level would not locate him.

He wrote:

> *Thursday, 2:09 AM. Coffee-buyer back. New circuit — covering my residential block. He has my address.*
>
> *My response: directional suppression active. Tested at full reduction. Effective against passive scan at the coffee-buyer's level. Unknown effectiveness against Elder Kuo, who appears to operate at higher resolution.*
>
> *Two weeks on the investigator's backup request. That is still the best estimate of when they find me.*
>
> *More urgent: Chen Yufei. The node collapse was two days ago. She is moving faster than the investigator, probably — she is local, she knows the area, and she is motivated by her own understanding of what a node collapse means rather than institutional protocols.*
>
> *I have been mapping her Qi signature since her first visit to the store. I know what she feels like at close range.*
>
> *I also know what I am going to say when she finds me.*
>
> *Tomorrow: back into the Strata. The presence to the north. I need what's in there before the surface situation closes in.*
>
> *There is not much time.*

He turned the lamp off at 3 AM and lay in the dark with his channels running quietly, the suppression active, thinking about what it meant to be found versus to present yourself.

He had been running on the premise of *be findable on your own terms.* He had been inside the Strata seven times. He had not yet encountered a confrontation he'd needed to navigate with another practitioner. He had not tested his framework against the reality of someone who trained inside a system that had been dealing with *unaffiliated practitioners* for centuries.

He would need to test it soon.

He had thought carefully about what he was going to say.

He went to sleep at 3:30 AM and slept until eight and woke feeling ready, which was either accurate or wishful and he would find out which.
