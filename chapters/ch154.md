# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Fifty-Four — Dimensional Weaver — Theory

He asked Axis-7 the next day, as planned.

The question of what the Layer 3 community wanted from a formalized surface relationship turned out to require a longer conversation than he'd anticipated — Axis-7's answer began with a redirect he hadn't expected.

*That question is premature,* she sent, when he asked. *The community's position on surface relationships depends partly on your own development. A Carrier at Compression Stage has different relevance to the surface than a Carrier at Dimensional Weaver Stage. The community is observing which one you will become before forming a position on what role you play in any formal arrangement.*

He held this.

*What is the difference, precisely?* he sent. He had a working understanding from the Scale Walking manual's occasional references, but he had never had it explained directly.

She walked him to the restricted section — not opening it, but standing before it, and explained at a pace he could follow.

*Compression is a discipline of the self. Every technique you have learned — Ratio 1 through Ratio 7 — concerns the practitioner's own spatial presence, their own interface with the Veritonal substrate. You compress yourself. You navigate space at the scale you have made yourself. The world around you does not change. You change relative to it.*

He nodded, though the gesture meant nothing to her register.

*Dimensional Weaving is a discipline of the world. A Weaver does not change their own scale to interact with the environment differently. A Weaver changes the environment's relationship to itself.* A pause. *Where a Compression practitioner navigates through a wall by becoming small enough that the wall's molecular structure has room for them, a Weaver can fold the space between two points so that the wall is, briefly and locally, not between them at all.*

He thought about this. The distinction was significant — not a difference of degree but of category, the same kind of qualitative shift he'd encountered between Ratio levels but applied across an entirely different axis.

*Why does this matter for the surface relationship question?*

*Because a Compression-stage Carrier is, fundamentally, a single practitioner with personal capability. A Dimensional Weaver-stage Carrier has the capability to affect structures beyond themselves — to repair the Under-Web at scale, to stabilize Strata boundaries, to potentially restore degraded nodes that Compression-level technique cannot reach.* A pause. *The dead node beneath your former workplace. You identified, months ago, that full coherence restoration requires Ratio 5. That estimate assumed Compression-stage technique. Dimensional Weaving offers an alternative approach that may not require Ratio 5 at all.*

He sat with the implications of this.

*If I develop Dimensional Weaving capability,* he sent, *the community's calculation about a surface relationship changes because I would be able to do things for the surface — and for them — that I currently cannot.*

*Yes.*

*How long does Dimensional Weaver development take?*

A long pause — Axis-7's version of careful consideration.

*Variable. The theoretical foundation alone — what is in this restricted section — typically requires one to two years of comprehension work before the practical technique becomes accessible. The practical development beyond that: decades, typically.* A pause. *You have been progressing faster than typical across every stage so far.*

He thought about Ratio 4, achieved in eleven months against what the manual suggested should take considerably longer. He thought about the communication register, the Colony Stage early development, the speed that had concerned even Axis-7 in earlier conversations.

*Faster is not always better,* she added, as if reading the direction of his thought, which perhaps in the Colony Link sense she partly was. *Speed without integration produces instability. The previous Carriers progressed more slowly and integrated more completely at each stage. You are choosing speed because external circumstances require it. This is a reasonable choice given your circumstances. It carries cost.*

*What cost?*

*Unknown precisely. Possibly nothing significant. Possibly instability in capabilities that have not had time to settle.* A pause. *I am watching for it. I would tell you if I saw it.*

He believed her.

He wrote, sitting in the corridor outside the restricted section, with the lock's two-dimensional architecture visible to him now and the third dimension still beyond his perception:

> *Dimensional Weaving: discipline of the world, not the self. Folds space rather than compressing the practitioner. Could offer alternative path to repairing the dead node without requiring Ratio 5.*
>
> *Theoretical foundation: 1–2 years comprehension work, typically. Practical development: decades, typically.*
>
> *I am progressing faster than typical at every stage. Axis-7 says this carries unknown cost. She is watching for instability.*
>
> *The community's position on a formal surface relationship is contingent on which stage I become. This makes my own development not just a personal project but a diplomatic variable.*
>
> *I had not understood this until today.*

He looked at the restricted section's door.

He had four months before his comprehension would meet its threshold, by Axis-7's earlier estimate.

He thought: I am going to need every one of those months, and I should use them carefully rather than rushing toward the door simply because the door is there.

He stood up.

He went back to his current work — the secondary lock practice, the annotation exchange with Meen-4, the translation document's ongoing replies.

The restricted section would still be there when he was ready.

He was, increasingly, comfortable with that kind of patience.
