# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Fifty-Five — Sunday Call — Different

His mother called at 10 AM.

He had been awake since six, working through the Compression geometry again. He had his notebooks spread across the desk and the Codex open on his phone and tea going cold at his elbow in the way that tea had gone cold at his elbow for as long as he could remember.

He picked up on the second ring.

"You sound awake," she said, which meant she'd expected him to sound less awake.

"I've been working."

"Since when?"

"Six."

A pause that contained her not-saying several things. "Are you sleeping?"

"Seven hours," he said, which was approximately true and represented improvement. "What's in the garden?"

"The last of the tomatoes." She sighed the sigh of someone who had watched good things end. "I should have picked them earlier. Your cousin Mei's wedding is in three weeks. You remember."

"I remember."

"Are you coming on the train?"

"Yes." He moved the cold tea to the edge of the desk. "How are you feeling?"

A brief pause — slightly different from the other pauses. "Fine. My back has been better. The doctor said the exercises were working."

He listened.

Not just to what she said. To how she said it. He had been developing this sensitivity since the Sunday call in chapter five, when he'd first noticed sub-verbal information in her voice. The Chitin Seed had been growing since then, and his emotional Qi sensitivity had grown with it, and what had been a faint frequency he could barely distinguish was now something closer to a full channel — not intrusive, not overwhelming, just clear.

What he heard, in the warmth between her words, was something he had not heard precisely before: she was proud of him.

Not for anything specific. Not for the geological consulting or the research or the choices he'd made. Just — proud, with the particular quality of pride that did not require knowing exactly what it was for, that was attached to the person rather than to any achievement of the person. She was proud of him the way she had probably been proud of him since before he was old enough to do anything worth being proud of.

He sat with this for a moment.

He said: "I've been doing something important."

She was quiet.

"I can't explain it fully yet," he said. "But I wanted you to know. It's — it matters. I think it matters a lot."

"I know," she said. Which was not what he expected. "I've known something was happening since the spring. You sound different."

"Different how?"

She considered. "Settled," she said. "The same word I used in the fall. But more settled now. Like you've found the ground under your feet."

He looked at his desk, at the notebooks and the Codex and the tea gone cold.

"That's close," he said.

She laughed, once, the warm sound he'd been hearing since childhood. "I'm your mother. I'm allowed to be metaphorical." A pause. "Whatever it is — do it well. Finish it properly."

"Yes," he said.

"And come to the wedding."

"I'll be there."

---

He sat with the phone in his hand after the call ended, not looking at anything in particular.

*She misses him, deeply and specifically.* That was what he'd heard, many calls ago, when the sensitivity was new. He had booked a train ticket.

*She is proud of him.* He hadn't known this the same way before. Not the intellectual knowledge — he'd always known she was proud of him in the way that mothers were proud. The direct perception of its quality in her voice, the specific texture of it.

He thought: the Chitin Seed is not just changing how I perceive the Veritonal network. It is changing how I perceive people.

He thought: this was always part of the system. The Colony Link was never only about the Under-Web. It was about distributed intelligence between living things. The ant colony reads the field between the ants. I am learning to read the field between people.

He wrote:

> *Sunday call. She is proud of me. I knew this intellectually. I perceived it directly.*
>
> *The Colony Link's biological dimension: not only Qi network perception but interpersonal field perception. The substrate isn't only geological. It runs through people too.*
>
> *This is the dimension of the Seed's growth I've been least prepared for. The Codex focuses on the geological/spatial layer. But people are also part of the system.*
>
> *What I feel about this: responsible. More responsible than I was yesterday.*

He capped the pen.

He booked a train ticket for his cousin's wedding.

He turned back to the Compression geometry.

He was going to find the ground under his feet, just as she'd said. The ground was forty meters below a convenience store in Wuhou District.

He was going to stand on it soon.
