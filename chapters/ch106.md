# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Six — The Dimensional Lock

He had been working on the dimensional lock geometry for three weeks when he understood what it actually was.

Not what it *did* — he'd known from the beginning that it was an access control mechanism, a seal that prevented opening without the correct combination of credentials and technique. What he hadn't understood until week three was what it was *made of.*

The lock existed at two scales simultaneously.

At Ratio 1 — his normal operating compression — the lock appeared as a sealed interface around the container, a Qi-dense boundary with a specific energy signature he could feel but not easily read. At Ratio 2, with the finer resolution of the deeper compression, the same lock appeared as something completely different: a geometric structure with interior architecture, rooms within rooms at the molecular scale, active processes running through the lock's material the way active processes ran through the Strata's walls.

The lock was not a static barrier. It was a dynamic system.

And unlocking it required operating at both scales simultaneously — feeling the Ratio-1 interface while manipulating the Ratio-2 interior, the way you might manipulate a mechanism through a wall by feeling the wall's response to your pressure.

This was the precursor to Dimensional Weaving. He didn't know that yet — he wouldn't have the Intermediate Archive's terminology for another month. But he was doing the conceptual work that made Dimensional Weaving comprehensible.

---

He had been building toward the dual-scale Chitin Circuit for two weeks. The basic circuit design he'd mastered; what he needed now was a circuit that could maintain two distinct Qi frequency outputs simultaneously — one tuned to the Ratio-1 interface, one tuned to the Ratio-2 interior — without the frequencies interfering with each other.

This was an inscription problem. The geometric notation for dual-frequency output required the two sets of channel circuits to run in parallel without sharing nodes, which meant the inscription density needed to be high enough that he was approaching the limits of what he could achieve at his current Compression accuracy.

He made four attempts.

The first three produced interference at varying levels — the two frequencies bleeding into each other, creating Qi noise that was actually worse than the lock's natural state.

The fourth attempt: he changed his approach entirely, thinking about it not as two circuits running simultaneously but as one circuit that *alternated* between frequencies at a rate fast enough to effectively maintain both. Like AC current rather than DC — not two wires but one wire that changed direction quickly enough to serve both purposes.

He inscribed the alternating-frequency circuit on his fifth board.

He held it to the lock.

The lock's Ratio-1 interface responded to the first frequency: a resonance, a slight softening of the boundary.

He pushed the circuit to Ratio-2 mode.

The lock's molecular interior responded to the second frequency: a shift in the geometric structure, a specific door opening in the room-within-room architecture.

He pushed back to Ratio-1 and applied pressure at the softened boundary.

The lock clicked open.

---

Not an actual click. A Qi-level equivalent — a release, the boundary dissolving, the container opening with a quality of relief that he could only describe as the way a held breath sounds when it's finally let out.

He held the container in both hands and looked at what was inside.

A preserved Formican cultivation manual, its cover inscribed with notation he could read clearly:

*Scale Walking: Practical Techniques for the Compression Practitioner.*

He set it on the library table.

He opened it.

Then he wrote in his notebook, before reading a single word of the manual:

> *Dimensional lock: opened. Method: dual-frequency alternating Chitin Circuit operating at both Ratio-1 and Ratio-2 simultaneously. The mechanism is bilateral scale operation — cannot be done from one scale alone.*
>
> *What this required: the ability to maintain awareness of two different Compression states and their effects simultaneously. This is a new capability. I could not have done this two months ago.*
>
> *I did not try two months ago. I did not know it existed.*
>
> *What the lock was testing: not just whether I had the credentials (Chitin Seed integration, communication register). Whether I had developed sufficient multi-scale awareness to operate across compression levels simultaneously. The lock is a comprehension test.*
>
> *Everything in this archive is a comprehension test.*
>
> *I should have expected this.*

He put the pen down.

He turned to page one of *Scale Walking: Practical Techniques.*

He read until the archive's ambient light began to feel like late afternoon rather than morning, which was not a reliable indicator of time (the Strata had no natural light), but was a reliable indicator that he had been reading for several hours.

He went home with the manual in his bag — it was transferable, the Chitin Seed unlocking portability — and ate dinner and slept and woke up the next morning still thinking about what he'd read.

The scope of what he hadn't known about Compression had just changed dramatically.
