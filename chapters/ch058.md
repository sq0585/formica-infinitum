# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Fifty-Eight — Return

On the train from Leshan, which had become, across two visits, a space associated with the particular thinking that came from sustained motion through a landscape he'd traveled his whole life without perceiving at the depth he could now —

He wrote:

> *I have been curious about the world.*
>
> *I am now curious about people.*
>
> *About what they carry that they don't say.*
>
> *My mother carried September for three months. Auntie Zhang carries her father's shop, demolished twenty years ago. The man in 4B carries something that drives him to argue with empty rooms at midnight. Ren Wuji carried four hundred years of solitude toward a transmission he couldn't be certain he'd manage.*
>
> *The Codex maps the geological substrate — the Under-Web, the Strata, the filament network through rock and earth. This is one layer. But people are also part of the system. The Colony Link runs through living things, not just through geology.*
>
> *What the ant colony shows: the field between individuals is not empty. Information moves through it. Decisions emerge from it.*
>
> *What people show: the field between people is also not empty. What they carry, what they don't say, what they feel under what they present — this is field-level information. It moves.*
>
> *I am beginning to be able to read it.*
>
> *This might be the most important thing the Seed is building. Not the perception of rock and filament. The perception of people.*
>
> *Not to use. To understand. The difference matters.*

He put the pen down and watched the hills and the lights through the window.

He thought about what it meant to understand something without needing to use that understanding. He had always known, intellectually, that understanding and use were separable. He was learning it at the level of practice.

He could perceive his mother's emotional field. He had not used this to manage her or manipulate the conversation or arrange outcomes. He had used it to listen more carefully, to ask the right question at the right moment, to receive what she offered when she offered it.

That was different.

He thought about the investigator, Elder Kuo Tianming, somewhere in Chengdu, who had methods for dealing with unaffiliated Qi sources. He thought about what it meant to be found by someone whose methods he didn't know. He thought about the threshold crossing he was planning for this week.

He thought: the cultivation path is not only about what I can perceive. It is about what I do with perception. At every scale.

The train arrived at Chengdu. He walked home.

He opened his notebook to the Compression geometry section.

Tomorrow morning. Before the investigator narrowed the radius any further.

The ground was below. Time to find it.
