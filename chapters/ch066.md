# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Sixty-Six — The Node Dead

It happened on a Tuesday, at 3:14 AM.

He was in the Strata — his fourth entry, two weeks after the first — when the node died.

He felt it rather than saw it: the subtle background Qi charge of the surface network, which had been a consistent presence even underground, a faint orientation point that told him which way was up and which way was north — it flickered. Then it wasn't there.

Not depleted. Gone. The difference between a lamp dimming and a lamp switching off.

He stood still in the vaulted hall and recalibrated. He could still navigate — the Strata's internal Qi field was independent of the surface node's charge, and his own channels were running on the deeper substrate, not the surface network. He was not in danger. He was simply now without the surface reference point, which felt different.

He noted the time. He exited through the threshold. He went home and ran a Qi survey.

The node beneath the 7-Eleven: absent. The filament network ran past the location where the node had been without the local concentration that had marked it for four months. The threads were intact — the depletion hadn't damaged the network itself, just the pool that had accumulated at that junction. The pool was gone. The rivers remained.

He wrote:

> *3:14 AM. Node dead. Confirmed by survey — network intact, local pool absent.*
>
> *Cause: cumulative depletion over five months. My own usage was the primary draw throughout. In the final three weeks, Elder Kuo's investigative scanning — wide-range Qi perception sweeps of the Wuhou area, conducted multiple times daily — added a secondary drain that accelerated the final collapse by approximately two weeks. Ren Wuji's four-month estimate was accurate for my usage rate alone; the investigator's arrival shortened the remaining window.*
>
> *Consequence: Chen Yufei's monitoring instruments have now registered a complete collapse. This event will be treated as a significant anomaly — nodes don't die on their own, especially not nodes that were healthy six months ago.*
>
> *Investigation escalation: certain. Timeline to direct contact: days.*
>
> *What I feel: the flash of guilt from earlier is back. Not stronger — clearer. I know what I caused. I know the mechanism. I have a complete account of how we got here.*
>
> *The account doesn't exonerate me. It clarifies what I am responsible for.*

Across the city, at a monitoring station in a building above a ley-line node in the Jinjiang district, instruments registered the collapse at 3:14 AM with a notation that would be reviewed by Chen Yufei at 7:30 AM the following morning.

She would look at the data.

She would look at the location.

She would remember the 7-Eleven, and the man who had said nothing unusual had happened.

She would begin making different plans.

---

Lin Mushi didn't know any of this yet.

What he knew was that the node was dead and the timeline had collapsed and the situation was now not a matter of weeks but a matter of days.

He had two options, as he saw them.

The first was to wait to be found — to let the investigation come to him, on the investigator's terms, in a situation he hadn't controlled.

The second was to be findable on his own terms, when he chose, in a way that established what he was before they could establish what they believed him to be.

He had been thinking about the response since Ren Wuji's audio file had told him: *think about the response before it arrives.*

He had thought about it. He had a response.

He opened his notebook and began drafting what he would say.

He would not wait.

He would present himself.
