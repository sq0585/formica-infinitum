# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Seventy-One — What He Carries

The seventh audio file unlocked with the auxiliary channel integration.

Ren Wuji's voice — dry, precise, faintly amused, more familiar to him now than the voice of anyone he'd actually spoken to in the past month — said:

"Seven. You are ahead of my expected pace. This is not necessarily good. Pace that outstrips comprehension creates gaps. I am not suggesting you slow down. I am suggesting you verify that what you have been moving quickly through has been genuinely understood, not merely performed."

A pause.

"The seventh file is about what you owe.

"Not to me. I am dead. You owe me nothing. What you owe is structural: what you learn, you owe to those who could learn it from you. This is not altruism. It is how the network maintains itself. The colony doesn't keep its route-finding capacity in any single ant. It keeps it in the relationship between ants — in the transfer of information across the field. If a practitioner learns and does not transmit, the capacity dies with them.

"I spent four hundred years learning. I transmitted it in four minutes. If I had died without transmitting, everything I learned would have been lost. I came close to dying without transmitting several times. Each time, I recognized that the closest available awake mind was not adequate to the transmission. I waited.

"You were not the optimal choice. There is no optimal choice, in practice — there is only the best available in the time remaining. You were curious, awake, present, and close. That was sufficient.

"What this means for you: when you have learned enough to transmit, you will need to find someone for whom you are the best available in the time remaining. You will not know in advance who this is. You will recognize them when the moment comes.

"Do not wait too long. I nearly did."

A longer pause.

"The debt is not abstract. It is specific: specific knowledge, specific capability, specific understanding — owed to specific people who could receive it. You will know who they are by the same quality you recognized in yourself when I chose you: awake enough. That is all. The rest follows."

The file ended.

---

Lin Mushi sat at his desk and looked at the wall above it, where he had pinned the first page of his first spatial survey sketch — the four pages of the vaulted hall, drawn at 5 AM on the morning after the architectural perception breakthrough.

*What you learn, you owe to those who could learn it from you.*

He had been thinking about this since the question-of-intent chapter, when he'd first articulated that the direction was embedded in what Ren Wuji had chosen to transmit. He had a partial answer now: not just the general direction of *share knowledge responsibly*, but a specific formulation of what that meant.

The Colony Link. The field between individuals through which information moved. The distributed capacity that existed not in any node but in the transfer.

He thought about Gao, who had said *okay* and listened. Who had backup thinking and whom he hadn't been talking to enough.

He thought about Chen Yufei, who was somewhere in the city with monitoring instruments that had registered a node collapse and was now moving toward him.

He thought about *ShenyueLab* on the physics forum, who had FAST telescope data that matched the Codex notation and had been waiting for a reply for two months.

He thought about the presence to the north, in the Strata, that had been patient for something.

He wrote:

> *Audio file 7. Core content: the debt. What you learn, you owe to those who could learn it from you.*
>
> *The debt is not abstract. It is specific.*
>
> *Who I owe:*
> *— The person to the north, who has been waiting for a particular kind of contact*
> *— Chen Yufei, who is about to find me and deserves to find someone who has thought about what to say*
> *— ShenyueLab, whose data I haven't responded to and who may be further along than I know*
> *— Gao, who I have been underutilizing as backup thinking*
>
> *Ren Wuji transmitted in his last four minutes. He nearly waited too long.*
>
> *I have been learning for five months. I should not wait much longer before beginning to transmit.*

He capped the pen.

He had a contact in the Strata to make and an investigator closing in and a month-old forum message to answer and a practitioner who was going to find him within days.

The transmission needed to start.

Tomorrow: back into the Strata. This time, he was going to walk toward the north.
