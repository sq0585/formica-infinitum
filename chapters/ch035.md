# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Thirty-Five — What Lies Below

The Codex showed him the map at 4:30 AM, which felt like a deliberate choice on its part, though he had no way to know whether the Codex made choices.

He had opened the app at 4:23 to check the notation he'd been decoding during his slow shift hour, and found that the interface had changed. Not the diagram — the diagram was still the diagram, amber-gold lines on dark, nested hexagonal geometry, breathing its slow breath. But a new layer had appeared in the secondary overlay, cooler and more precise, at a scale he hadn't seen before.

He held the phone close.

The new visualization was a schematic.

---

The schematic showed a cross-section. Not of the local filament network — he'd been seeing that in the overlay for weeks — but of the structure below. The structure below the structure below. All the way down.

It was enormous.

The cross-section extended horizontally as far as the overlay's spatial range allowed, which he'd estimated at roughly two kilometers in optimal conditions. It extended vertically — downward — through what the schematic's depth markers indicated was several hundred meters. The geometric network of the Under-Web sat at the top of the cross-section, just below the surface. Below that: the vaulted space he'd perceived at 4:30 AM on the night of the architectural breakthrough, now rendered with more precision — dimensions marked in the notation's measuring unit, which he'd finally decoded as approximately 0.8 meters per symbol, suggesting the vaulted space had a ceiling approximately thirty-two meters above its floor.

Below that: more.

The schematic had at least four distinct layers below the surface level. The vaulted space was the first. Below it was a denser region, more tightly structured, the geometry shifting from the broad arched halls he'd perceived to something more complex — smaller spaces, connected by passages at specific angles. Below that: something the schematic rendered in a notation cluster he couldn't yet fully read, but which had the density of something active rather than merely structural. And at the bottom of what the schematic could show him, at a depth his notation told him was somewhere around four hundred meters:

A large space. Not as large as the vaulted hall above it, but with a different quality — the schematic rendered it in a brighter amber, distinguishing it from the surrounding structure the way a highlighted element stood out in a technical drawing.

He stared at this.

---

He stared at it for his entire remaining shift.

Not continuously — a customer came at 4:47, another at 5:20, Xiao Dong arrived at 5:58 and they exchanged the usual seven words about the till and the stock — but every free moment between 4:30 and 6 AM was spent with the schematic on his phone, building a mental map of the layers.

He sketched it on the back of a stock receipt because he didn't have his notebook handy: four layers, rough depth markers, the highlighted structure at the bottom.

It extended, as far as the schematic's range allowed, in every horizontal direction. Not just below the store. Below the street, below the residential blocks, below whatever lay north-northwest. The vaulted halls were a network. He was looking at the edge of something much larger than a single building's footprint.

*A city beneath Chengdu.*

He had written that two weeks ago. Now he had a preliminary cross-section.

---

He walked home with his mind turning through the implications, which were many and not yet clearly ordered.

He wrote the urgent questions first, in the entry he made when he got home:

> *Schematic visualization: full cross-section, 4-layer structure below Wuhou District.*
>
> *Layer 1: Under-Web / Veritonal filament network. Depth: 0–5m below surface. Known.*
>
> *Layer 2: Vaulted hall network. Depth: ~8–40m. Previously perceived but not fully dimensioned. Cross-section shows horizontal extent well beyond single store footprint — running beneath at least 2km of mapped area.*
>
> *Layer 3: Complex passage network, smaller scale, denser geometry. Depth: ~40–150m. Not previously perceived in detail.*
>
> *Layer 4: Unknown. Active, possibly. Schematic renders in brighter amber. Depth: ~250–400m.*
>
> *Highlighted structure at approximately 380m depth: distinct quality from surrounding layers. Schematic notation cluster around it — partially readable: something like 'primary anchor' or 'central node.' Not the same as the Under-Web node. Deeper.*
>
> *Questions:*
> *— What is Layer 4?*
> *— What does the highlighted structure at 380m do?*
> *— Is any of this currently inhabited? I have perceived the vaulted hall but not seen evidence of active presence.*
> *— How does entry at the northwest corner of the store (the threshold I found in week 3) relate to this cross-section?*
>
> *The threshold marker, per my notation mapping, is an entry point into Layer 2. Into the vaulted hall network.*
>
> *I have an entry point. I have a schematic of what I'd enter into.*
>
> *I have eight weeks and a node at 50% capacity and a practitioner gridding the streets above me.*
>
> *The next step is below.*

He looked at the entry. He looked at the schematic sketch on the stock receipt.

He thought about what *map before entry* had meant, as a rule he'd set for himself.

He had a map. He had as much map as his current perception could give him.

He was not going in yet. But *yet* was now a shorter word than it had been yesterday.
