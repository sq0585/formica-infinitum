# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter Three Hundred and Fifty — The Response to the Objection

He took three days before responding formally, wanting Wei Canglan's objection to receive the full, deliberate consideration it deserved rather than a quick answer offered simply to move the process forward.

He spent the first day alone, working through the reasoning methodically, testing his own conclusion against every angle Wei Canglan's document had raised. He spent the second day in conversation — Chen Yufei first, then Liang Jingzhi, then a longer session with Fang Ruxue, whose careful governance instincts brought a structural perspective none of the others had quite offered.

"The core question isn't really about Amber-0," Fang Ruxue observed, during that second conversation. "It's about whether removing a legal and practical defense is ever the right choice, even when the person removing it does so with full understanding and genuine conviction. That's a question with implications well beyond this specific system."

"What do you think the answer is?"

"I think it depends entirely on why the defense is being removed," she said. "If you're removing it to escape accountability for something you'd rather not own, that's reckless. If you're removing it because you've concluded the defense was never honest to begin with — that Amber-0's decisions were always, in some meaningful sense, extensions of your own judgment, formalized rather than independently generated — then removing it isn't recklessness. It's honesty catching up to a truth that was already there."

He sat with that framing, understanding it as close to his own reasoning as anything he'd yet heard articulated back to him.

---

Meen-4 joined the third day's conversation, present for the final, careful working-through of exactly how to respond to Wei Canglan's formal objection.

*I have been thinking about this as well,* she signaled, once the practical framework had settled. *I would like to offer something, if it is useful. I have spent four hundred years as this Strata's Keeper, and considerable time, more recently, reconsidering my own relationship to observation versus genuine participation — the Amber Lattice School, the history project, the Ring repair. I find that pattern relevant here.*

"What pattern?"

*The distinction between watching carefully from outside, and choosing, deliberately, to be genuinely inside something and accountable to it.* She paused, the particular stillness he'd learned to recognize as careful, considered honesty. *I have observed your development for two years now, closely, as Keeper. I find that observation alone no longer feels like an adequate description of what I actually am to you, or to this city, or to whatever comes next in the review. I would like that to be reflected honestly, the same way you are asking Amber-0's role to be reflected honestly.*

He understood, sitting with her words, that she was offering something considerably larger than commentary on his decision — though she offered it with such characteristic understatement that he almost missed the full shape of it in the moment itself.

"I hear you," he said simply, and let the conversation continue without further remark, understanding, somehow, that this wasn't a moment that needed ceremony to be real.

---

He drafted his formal response to Wei Canglan that evening, careful and complete.

*I have heard your objection fully, and I want to say clearly: you are right that integration removes a defense. I do not dispute that. I have concluded, after careful consideration involving Fang Ruxue, Chen Yufei, Liang Jingzhi, and Meen-4, that the defense was already less real than it appeared — that Amber-0's decisions have always been, in substance, formalized extensions of my own judgment, and that continuing to treat them as separate was a convenient fiction rather than an honest boundary.*

*I am proceeding with integration. I am doing so with your objection fully considered, not dismissed, and I want the record to reflect that your caution was heard, weighed seriously, and remains valid even though I have reached a different conclusion. I would like your continued vigilance going forward, exactly as careful and exactly as willing to speak plainly as it has been throughout this entire process.*

He sent it, and found, sitting with the decision now formally made, that the weight of it settled into him with the same steady clarity every genuine, carefully considered choice in this world had eventually settled into — not lightly, not without cost, but honestly, with open eyes, and with people around him who had made certain, through careful, principled friction, that he'd truly earned the right to call it his own.

He noted, briefly, in his journal that night, that Meen-4 had been present for nearly every significant conversation of the past several months — the review preparation, the Ring project, and now this — a presence so steady it had become, somewhere along the way, simply part of the shape of things, unremarked and entirely trusted.
