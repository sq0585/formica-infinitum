# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Twenty-Six — Month Ten

Ten months in, he assessed.

He did this at the desk on a Wednesday morning, with a cold cup of tea to his left and the circuit network reading nominal on his phone to his right, and the specific attention he brought to assessments that he was about to do something irreversible with.

---

>
> *Compression: Ratio 3 stable in Strata (20 minutes), approaching stable on surface (currently 8 minutes maximum in low-density ambient). Ratio 2: fully stable anywhere, 45-minute maximum. Dual-scale awareness: natural operating mode.*
>
> *Channels: 15 primary, 19 auxiliary confirmed. Hundreds of secondary channels visible at Ratio 3 — not individually manageable but running as a distributed system.*
>
> *Colony Link: room-scale group emotional perception. Formican community below: perceptible as collective field from surface. Increasing range and specificity.*
>
> *Codex: Documents 1–6 complete. Document 7: accessible with Ratio 3 capability (just met threshold). Beginning first read.*
>
> *Communication register: 88% fluency. Adequate for complex exchanges with Axis-7.*
>
> *Chitin Circuits: third generation design complete. 12 units deployed (7 district network, 2 Strata, 1 at Gao's building, 1 with Elder Zhao, 1 portable). Manufacturing time per unit: 4 hours.*
>
> *Archive progress: outer hall mapped, Secondary Archive partially catalogued, Volume 1 of Predecessor Notes read. Intermediate Archive accessible (Meen-4's offer exchange ongoing — I give annotated translations, I receive deeper access).*
>
> *Node repair: 4 partially depleted nodes in the district Under-Web repaired to full coherence. The dead 7-Eleven node: not yet repairable at my current Ratio. Requires Ratio 5 for full coherence restoration. Filed as future work.*

>
> *Translation document: complete. Distribution list: 47 recipients. Release: pending Lady Shen's Strata entry.*

He read this back.

He added:

> *What I didn't know at month one that I know now:*
>
> *There is a predecessor's record in the archive. I am part of a lineage. The Formicans have been waiting for this — not waiting for me specifically, but waiting for a Carrier, and managing the wait for at least 180 years.*
>
> *The Strata is inhabited. The Formican community is substantial. My relationship to them is developing on their schedule as much as mine.*
>
> *The network ping has gone out. Something, somewhere, has been notified. Lead time: unknown.*
>
> *What I should have known at month one that I didn't:*
>
> *The node was inhabited-adjacent. My depletion affected real beings. I would have been more careful if I'd known.*
>
> *I know now. I'm being more careful now.*
>
> *Rule: the most important corrections are the ones you make after you understand what you did wrong.*

He capped the pen.

He opened Document 7 of the Codex.

The document's title: *Colony Stage — Integration with Distributed Intelligence Systems.*

He had been building toward this for ten months without knowing what he was building toward.

He began reading.
