# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Four — Axis-7's Report

He didn't know about the report until much later.

At the time it was filed — a Formican communication through the Strata's internal network, moving at a speed he couldn't have tracked even if he'd known to look for it — he was on the surface, eating lunch at a noodle shop near his apartment and reviewing his transcription notes. Chen Yufei was across the city attending to practical matters that had accumulated during the weeks she'd been spending increasing amounts of time underground. The archive was running its quiet maintenance cycles without either of them.

The report reached the Isolationist Elder Council in the Strata's Layer 3 approximately two hours after the meeting with the older Keeper.

What Axis-7 wrote — he would eventually be able to read this, when his communication register reached sufficient fluency, in the Secondary Archive's record of cross-layer communications:

*Report to Elder Council, Layer 3 Residence, from Archive Keeper Station, Chengdu Outer Layer.*

*A human has entered the outer library using Compression technique of an unusual geometric derivation — not consistent with any known Echo Court framework, not consistent with the degraded transmission lines that reach surface practitioners through the Under-Web's ambient field. The geometry is consistent with direct Codex access.*

*The human carries an active Chitin Seed. First integration cohort, based on signature characteristics — the Seed has been active for approximately 8 months by biological integration markers.*

*The human is accompanied, on subsequent visits, by a second human carrying a dormant Seed-adjacent Qi structure. This second human is likely a surface cultivation practitioner who has entered into relationship with the Seed-carrier.*

*The human has initiated communication attempts in the station's register. Current comprehension estimate: 60–70%, rising. The communication attempts are methodical and systematic.*

*The human has received Volume 1 of the Predecessor Notes from Archive Staff Member 7-Meen. I was not consulted on this transfer. I am noting it here.*

*Recommendation to Elder Council: formal observation status. No immediate action. The Seed-carrier's behavior pattern — systematic documentation, methodical communication, no apparent exploitation of access — is consistent with legitimate Inheritance Protocol activation. Containment preparation should be maintained as contingency but should not be deployed preemptively.*

*Assessment of threat level: low. Assessment of strategic significance: high.*

The Elder Council's response came back in eighteen hours: *Maintain observation. Prepare contingency. Report if behavior pattern shifts.*

Axis-7 filed the confirmation.

---

Lin Mushi, on the surface, finished his noodles and went home to work on the dimensional lock geometry.

He had no awareness of the report, the Elder Council, or the contingency preparation.

What he had was the lock's behavior under his latest test — a more sophisticated approach using the dual-scale Chitin Circuit he'd been designing for the past week — and a promising result: the lock had yielded to a different angle of approach than he'd been trying, suggesting the multi-dimensional aspect of its mechanism was oriented along an axis he'd been treating as secondary.

He adjusted the Circuit's geometric inscriptions.

He noted in his log: *Dimensional lock: progress. Orientation correction. Testing tomorrow.*

He made tea.

He did not know he was being monitored by a civilization that had been waiting two hundred years for someone exactly like him to walk through the door, and that the civilization's response was currently split between *legitimate Inheritance Protocol activation* and *contingency.* He did not know that a specific individual named Axis-7 had filed a report specifically noting that she had not been consulted about the notebook transfer.

He would learn all of this later, when his communication register was good enough to read the cross-layer archive.

Later would be fine.

For now: the lock. The geometry. The work.

He opened his notebook and kept going.
