# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Two Hundred and Fifty-Eight — Gou Ping's Escalation

He felt the ambush before he saw it, the particular instinct two years of accumulated danger had trained into him registering wrongness in the alley's geometry a half-second before his eyes confirmed it.

He'd left the convenience store late, the quiet walk home along a route he'd used dozens of times without incident, when the four Qi signatures resolved out of the evening shadows with the coordinated precision of people who had planned this specific encounter carefully and waited, patiently, for exactly this kind of unguarded moment.

Four cultivators, Compression Stage by the depth of their signatures, positioned to block every reasonable exit from the narrow alley he'd been walking through.

This wasn't the Collective. This wasn't the Strata. This was him, alone, on an ordinary street, no Spatial Anchor Array deployed, no days of preparation, no Chen Yufei or Wei Canglan moving in to help carry the load. Just four trained attackers and the particular cold clarity of understanding, all at once, exactly what kind of encounter this actually was.

This was not surveillance. This was not a clumsy attempt at capture, the kind Lady Shen had once nearly managed and then walked away from with something like genuine regret. The positioning was too clean, too coordinated, the four signatures arranged with the deliberate precision of people who had studied his habits, his usual route home, the specific hour when the street emptied enough to make an encounter like this possible without witnesses.

This was the first time someone had tried to kill him specifically, with intent and preparation, rather than simply attempting to capture, contain, or disrupt something he was protecting.

He recognized Gou Ping's particular signature among the four — sharper, more controlled than it had been during the chaotic assault on the Strata months ago, the unmistakable mark of someone who had spent the intervening time training specifically for a rematch he intended to win cleanly this time.

The nearest attacker shifted his weight, the subtle pre-motion of a technique about to launch, aimed with the kind of precision that suggested center-mass, lethal intent rather than disabling caution.

Lin Mushi had perhaps half a second before it landed.

He used it.
