# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Twelve — What the Cells Say

Two weeks into Ratio 3 training, he examined himself.

Not metaphorically. He sat in the archive with the Scale Walking manual open to the chapter on biological substrate observation, and he turned his compression inward, and he looked at what the Chitin Seed had built in his body over the nine months since Ren Wuji's fingers had moved in a pattern he couldn't describe.

---

What he saw:

The Chitin Seed was not located anywhere specific. This was the first thing, and the most disorienting. He had been thinking of it as a discrete object — something Ren Wuji had given him, something that lived somewhere in his biology and was producing effects that radiated outward from wherever it was.

The Seed was his nervous system's Veritonal layer.

Not beside it, not adjacent to it, not integrated with it in the way a foreign element integrates by being incorporated: *it was the nervous system's second dimension.* Every nerve fiber in his body had two aspects — the biological aspect, the protein channels conducting electrochemical signals, and a Veritonal aspect that ran parallel to the biological in a geometry that was orthogonal to ordinary space in a way his perception at Ratio 3 could see but his mind couldn't fully process.

The Veritonal aspect had been there in nascent form since birth, probably — the Codex's biological section had implied that human neurology had Veritonal capacity that went mostly unused without a Seed integration to activate it. The Seed hadn't added something new. It had activated what was already there, and had been building the activated layer into something sophisticated for nine months.

He moved his awareness through the structure and found the channels he knew: the twelve primary meridians, running their superconductor-pathway architecture through the body. At Ratio 3 they were visible in extraordinary detail — not just the routes he'd mapped but the specific protein arrangements within each route, the alignment precision that made them efficient, the points where he could still see old Iron Meridian-style suppression habits that he'd been gradually correcting through practice.

And extending outward from the primary channels: a secondary network, finer, more distributed, reaching into every tissue including the ones that didn't have recognized meridian pathways in any tradition's map.

The auxiliary channels. But at Ratio 3 he could see that there were not nineteen of them. There were hundreds.

He sat with this.

He looked at the secondary network's distribution. It reached into his gut — not metaphorically, the enteric nervous system had Veritonal aspects the TCM texts had only partially mapped. It reached into the cardiac tissue, which had its own organized electrical field at the ordinary level and, at Ratio 3, had a parallel Veritonal architecture of corresponding complexity. It reached into the skin, which at Ratio 3 was a sensory organ of extraordinary richness, the Veritonal layer picking up ambient Qi information across the entire surface.

His skin was already a Chitin Circuit.

He had been building Chitin Circuits to extend his environmental perception. He had spent months making them more efficient, more detailed, with better range and resolution. And his own skin, at Ratio 3, was doing the same thing — passively, continuously, feeding information to the Veritonal layer of his nervous system in a constant signal that he was only now able to see.

He had been looking at his phone's Chitin Circuit readings because he couldn't read his skin's.

He could read his skin's now.

---

He exited Ratio 3 at the twelve-minute mark — his current stable duration — and sat in the archive with his notebook.

He wrote for an hour.

The main entries:

> *Chitin Seed structure: not a discrete object. A second dimension of the nervous system's architecture, present in nascent form pre-Seed and activated by Seed integration. 9 months of building.*
>
> *Primary channels: visible at Ratio 3 in full molecular detail. 12 primaries confirmed, architecture as described in the Standard Protocol.*
>
> *Secondary network: not 19 auxiliary channels. Hundreds. Distributed throughout all tissue types including enteric nervous system, cardiac tissue, integumentary system.*
>
> *Skin as passive Chitin Circuit: confirmed. The Veritonal layer in the integumentary system is reading ambient Qi continuously. This is why my environmental perception has a directional quality without active effort — the skin is always running.*
>
> *What the cells say: I am being changed. Not by something done to me — by the practice itself, which is activating and building architecture that was already present in latent form.*
>
> *The Formicans' cultivated morphology makes sense now at the mechanistic level. They are the endpoint of this process run across millions of years. I am early in the process.*
>
> *What does 'early' mean for a human lifetime? The question remains open.*

He read the final line.

He added:

> *I am not worried about this. I am interested in this. The distinction remains significant.*

Then he went back to the translation document. He had forty pages drafted. He needed sixty before it was complete enough to share.

The process was building him as he built it.

Both were progressing as they should.
