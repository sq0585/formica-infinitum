# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Twenty-Eight — What the Document Says

The document was a manual.

He had suspected this — the *procedural prefix* symbol appeared throughout, and the rhythm markers organized everything into discrete phases — but seeing it legible was different from inferring it was probably legible. What had been opaque notation was now technical writing. Dense, precise, unsentimental technical writing, the kind produced by someone who assumed the reader had a working vocabulary and did not need encouragement.

He spent twelve hours reading it. He took notes on every page.

---

The full title, as best he could render it into Chinese: *Veritonal Flow Channeling — Standard Protocol for Biological Integration at Micro Scale.*

The word he was rendering as *Micro Scale* appeared in parentheses after a longer notation sequence that he could now read as something like *the first and most fundamental scale of perception, accessible to practitioners at the beginning stage of Chitin Seed integration.* The document was, in other words, beginner's material. He had been working with beginner's material for a month and only now could read it.

He did not find this discouraging. He found it clarifying.

---

The Protocol described, across ten phases with rhythm markers indicating pace, how a practitioner in early Chitin Seed integration should establish what it called a *stable interface* with the Veritonal network. Not a connection — an interface. The distinction mattered: a connection implied two separate things joined, whereas an interface implied a negotiated boundary that served both sides.

Phase One: *Open Perception.* Done. He had been in Phase One since Day One.

Phase Two: *Confirm Directionality.* He had done this accidentally when he'd established the north-northwest heading of the depth sensation. Done.

Phase Three: *Acknowledge the Substrate.* The bilateral exchange. The handshake. He had not done this deliberately.

Phase Four: *Establish Breath Regulation.* A specific breathing pattern, described in the notation with rhythm markers indicating cadence: four counts in, two hold, six counts out, two hold. Repeated. He recognized the shape of this from his brief research into traditional qigong. Someone had preserved a fragment.

Phases Five through Eight: a series of increasingly fine-grained attention exercises — directing perception to specific points in the body along pathways he was now confident matched the meridian channels of traditional Chinese medicine, the biological superconductor network of the biochemist's paper, the routing architecture of the Chitin Seed's integration into his nervous system.

Phase Nine: *First Channel Opening.* The document was specific: this phase should not be attempted until Phases One through Eight had been practiced consistently for a minimum period measured in what he was translating as *anchor cycles*, a unit he didn't yet have a firm conversion for.

Phase Ten: *Verification and Recording.* Document what happened. Note what changed. Repeat from Phase Four until stable.

He wrote the summary and read it back.

---

> *Document 2 summary:*
>
> *The Standard Protocol is exactly that — standard. Foundational. What I've been doing intuitively matches Phases 1 and 2. Phase 3 (acknowledge the substrate) is the first thing I haven't done. Phases 4–8 are structured training practices. Phase 9 is the first deliberate channel opening.*
>
> *Current status: I have already opened what appears to be at least one channel — the wrist healing suggests direct Qi direction through a biological pathway. But I don't know if I did it correctly, and I have not verified which channel it was.*
>
> *The document has a verification protocol for Phase 9 that involves specific sensory landmarks. I should run this on what I've already done before attempting anything new.*
>
> *Estimated time to Phase 9 (following the protocol as specified): unknown, pending translation of 'anchor cycle' unit.*
>
> *What the document says, plainly: the Formicans did not teach Micro Scale as a power to acquire. They taught it as a sensory mode to inhabit. The entire protocol is about perception, not capability. You learn to perceive clearly first. Everything else follows from what you can see.*

He set the notebook down.

Outside his window, a pigeon landed on the power line and sat in the attitude of an entity that had made its peace with uncertainty.

*The Formicans did not teach it as a power to acquire.* He underlined this in the notebook and starred it.

This was what the man had chosen to transmit. Not a power. A way of seeing. The power, when it came, would be a consequence of seeing correctly.

He thought about the ant colony question that he had underlined three times in his old notebooks. The colony that made decisions no individual ant initiated.

The colony didn't acquire decision-making. It assembled the conditions in which decision-making emerged.

He turned to a fresh page.

*Phase Three: Acknowledge the Substrate.*

Tonight.
