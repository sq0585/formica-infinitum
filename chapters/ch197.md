# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Ninety-Seven — What Comes Next

He sat with the new Intermediate Archive materials for two days before setting them aside to think about something more immediate.

The political situation, for the moment, had stabilized — the ultimatum withdrawn, the suppression coalition fracturing, the assembly's loose structure beginning to take its first tentative shape. But he understood, with the clear-eyed strategic assessment he'd developed over the past sixteen months, that stabilization was not the same as resolution.

He wrote in his notebook, working through the shape of what came next:

> *Strategic assessment: the information war has gone, on balance, well. The documents are accurate, increasingly verified, increasingly accepted even by institutions that initially resisted. Lady Shen's direct action failed and cost the aggressive faction credibility. The Iron Meridian's internal split continues to widen in favor of acknowledgment over suppression.*
>
> *But the information war has a timeline I haven't been respecting carefully enough. Truth, once released, doesn't stay static in how it's received. The longer the current uncertain equilibrium continues — Courts neither fully accepting nor fully rejecting, an assembly with no defined structure, Lady Shen recalculating but not yet committed to a new approach — the more opportunity exists for someone less careful than I've tried to be to fill the gap with something worse.*
>
> *Axis-7's Amber Lattice School precedent gave me a model. I haven't built it yet. I have an assembly, a shared channel, and good intentions. That's not yet a functioning structure.*
>
> *What I actually need, I think, is to reach Dimensional Weaver capability — not for combat purposes, though the confrontation with Lady Shen made clear that capability gap matters more than I'd like to admit. I need it because Axis-7 told me, months ago, that a Dimensional Weaver-stage Carrier has capability to affect structures beyond themselves — to repair the Under-Web at scale, to stabilize the Strata's boundaries, to do things for the surface and for the Layer 3 community that Compression-stage technique cannot reach.*
>
> *If I'm going to build something like the Amber Lattice School's model — a structure that doesn't require exclusive institutional authority, built on demonstrated capability and genuine community — I think it needs to be backed by genuine capability to do useful things at scale, not just accurate information and careful diplomacy.*
>
> *The intellectual battle has a timeline. I need to reach Dimensional Weaver before that timeline reaches its endpoint — before the current uncertain equilibrium resolves itself in a direction I haven't shaped, simply because I was still building theoretical foundation while events moved without me.*

He read this back.

He thought about the restricted section's lock, the two dimensions of its architecture he could already perceive, the third still beyond him. He thought about the foundational theory in the Intermediate Archive's third tier, still partially unread. He thought about the months Axis-7 had estimated for the theoretical foundation alone — one to two years, typically, though he had been progressing faster than typical at every previous stage.

He thought about the cost Axis-7 had warned him about — speed without integration, instability in capabilities that hadn't had time to settle.

He weighed this against the strategic timeline he'd just written out.

He made a decision.

He would not abandon caution. But he would not let caution become an excuse for drift, either.

He turned to the restricted section's third dimension, the piece of the lock's architecture he hadn't yet managed to perceive clearly, and began, with renewed focus, the work of understanding it.

He had a timeline to meet, and not unlimited time to meet it in.
