# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Forty-Five — The Investigator Arrives

Elder Kuo Tianming checked into a hotel two kilometres from the 7-Eleven on a Thursday.

Lin Mushi did not know this yet.

What he knew was that something had changed in the city's Qi field — a new presence, disciplined and directed, moving through Wuhou District with a methodical attention that was qualitatively different from the coffee-buying practitioner who had been gridding the area for weeks. The coffee-buyer was a junior scout: his Qi signature was serviceable, his range limited, his purpose documentary rather than investigative. He gathered data and reported upward. He had never been the threat.

This was not him. This was someone with significantly more range, more precision, more depth.

He registered the new presence on Friday morning, when it came within his fifty-meter awareness radius during his walk to buy rice.

He stopped at the corner and paid attention.

The signature was controlled in a way that suggested decades of practice — not the clean, self-taught organization of his own channels, which he suspected looked structurally unusual to an external observer, but the well-worn discipline of someone who had trained within a framework for a very long time. Deeply familiar with their own capabilities, and therefore deeply aware of what was unusual about others.

The signature moved past his position at about thirty metres, heading north. It did not slow or orient toward him.

He bought his rice and went home and wrote:

> *Friday. New practitioner in area. This is not the coffee-buyer — the coffee-buyer has been a junior data-gatherer, reporting what he sees. This signature is orders of magnitude more refined. Decades of practice. This is the senior investigator the junior observer's reports have brought to Chengdu.*
>
> *The coffee-buyer was always reporting to someone. That someone is now here.*
>
> *Estimated range on the investigator: much wider than the coffee-buyer. If he extends his perception within 15 metres of me while I have channels active, detection becomes likely.*
>
> *The timeline has moved. This is the investigator Ren Wuji warned about.*
>
> *Revised estimate: weeks, not months.*
>
> *Response options: (1) reduce channel activity when outside. Risk: limits my ability to monitor him. (2) increase range and precision to maintain awareness of his location. Risk: my own increased activity may be perceptible. (3) begin the threshold crossing before he narrows his search.*
>
> *Option 3 is the correct answer. I have 9 channels. The minimum for safe threshold entry is 7. I'm past minimum. I am not at the complete 12-channel circuit required for Compression, but the threshold crossing does not require Compression — it requires stable entry at Ratio 1.5, which 9 channels supports. I can cross the threshold now and continue opening the remaining channels while I operate inside.*
>
> *Decision: cross the threshold this week. Map enough of Layer 2 to have somewhere to go if the surface becomes untenable.*

He read this back.

He thought about the word *untenable*, which he had written without considering it. It implied a circumstance in which his apartment, his neighbourhood, his ordinary surface life could become a place he couldn't operate from. He had not previously let himself think that far forward.

He thought about it now.

He thought: if the investigator identifies me as the source of the node depletion, what happens next depends entirely on what the Northern Peak Court's investigator is authorized to do. He doesn't know yet. He needs to know.

He opened the Codex and navigated to the section on the Echo Courts, which he'd been able to access since the third document unlocked. He read for an hour.

What he found was not reassuring.

The Northern Peak Court had, in the Codex's historical records, a consistent pattern of aggressive acquisition of what they termed *unaffiliated Qi resources*. The phrase *unaffiliated Qi resource* appeared to cover: nodes, practitioners, and artifacts that operated outside Court authority structures. The acquisition methods documented ranged from negotiation (rare) to coercion (common) to what the notation described in terms that translated as *enforced integration*.

He read *enforced integration* twice.

He added to his earlier note:

> *Addendum: do not let the investigator find me before I understand what finding me means to him. The Court's historical approach to unaffiliated practitioners is not one I want applied to me without context or preparation.*
>
> *Channel 10: tonight. Then the threshold.*

He put the phone down. He made tea. He sat with the specific quality of focus that arrived when the timeline stopped being abstract.

The investigator was in the city.

He had a week, possibly less.

He had work to do.
