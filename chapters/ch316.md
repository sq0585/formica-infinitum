# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter Three Hundred and Sixteen — Meen-4's Full Disclosure

Axis-7 arrived at the administrative chamber the following morning, and Lin Mushi understood, watching the careful gravity with which Meen-4 greeted her, that whatever came next in this conversation had been building toward this moment for longer than the three days since he'd first noticed the node's inbound signal.

*You should tell him,* Axis-7 signaled, once the three of them had settled, her attention on Meen-4 with an intensity that felt less like deference than mutual agreement already reached. *He has found the signal himself. Withholding the context now serves nothing except your own comfort.*

Meen-4 was quiet for a moment, and he watched her make the specific decision he'd seen her make before — the deliberate choice to give him something heavy rather than continue protecting him from its weight.

*The Tibetan Witness Node is not a Formican installation in the sense you have assumed,* she signaled finally. *It was not built to observe this world for our own purposes. It was built to be observed. It is a monitoring point — one of several, we believe, though we have only ever confirmed this one — maintained by a body considerably larger than the Formican Empire ever was.*

He felt something go very still in his chest. "A larger body."

*The Galactic Collective,* Meen-4 signaled, the name arriving with a weight that suggested it had been waiting, carefully, for exactly this moment to finally be spoken aloud. *A coalition of civilizations, considerably older and more widespread than the Formican Empire, that maintains observation over worlds designated for restricted status. Earth has held such a designation for approximately forty million years.*

He sat with the sentence, its full implications arriving in pieces too large to process all at once.

"Since Veth-Karun," he said slowly.

*Yes.*

---

"You've known this the entire time," he said, not accusatory, simply working through the sequence of everything he'd learned across two years, trying to understand how deep the silence had actually gone. "Every reference to the Isolationists, every careful mention of consequences the Formicans faced after Veth-Karun's dissolution — this was underneath all of it."

*Yes,* Meen-4 signaled again, offering no defense, no softening. *I did not tell you earlier because the information was not actionable earlier, and because I did not believe you needed to carry the weight of a Collective's judgment before you had built anything the judgment might reasonably weigh. You have now built a great deal. The Strata City. The Collective — your Collective, the human and Formican one, distinct from the one I am now describing, though the naming overlap is unfortunate and I would ask you to hold the distinction carefully.* A pause. *You have also, as of three days ago, developed the capability to perceive the monitoring directly. That changes what withholding the information would mean. Continued silence, at this point, would not be protection. It would be deception.*

*The Witness Node's reactivation — the inbound signal you detected — indicates active attention,* Axis-7 added. *Not merely passive observation, the way the node has functioned for most of the past forty million years. Something has changed, recently, in how closely this world is being watched.*

"What triggered it?"

*We do not know with certainty,* Meen-4 signaled. *But I would draw your attention to the timeline. The inbound signal has been active for approximately three years. Three years ago, you inherited the Codex. Three years ago, this world's rate of Formican-derived cultivation development began accelerating beyond anything it had achieved in the preceding several centuries.*

He felt the weight of the implication settle fully.

"It's watching because of me."

*Not exclusively because of you,* Axis-7 signaled, something careful in the correction. *But your arrival, and everything that has followed from it, is almost certainly part of what drew renewed attention to a world that had, until three years ago, been quiet enough to warrant only passive monitoring.*

---

He sat with the full shape of it for a long while, the administrative chamber quiet around the three of them, and found, working through the specific vertigo of the revelation, that his mind kept returning not to fear, but to a kind of careful, methodical accounting — the same discipline that had carried him through every threshold before this one.

"What does the Collective actually do," he asked finally, "with a world it's watching?"

*I do not have a complete answer,* Meen-4 signaled. *The Formican Empire's own historical relationship with the Collective was limited, and what records exist are old enough that I cannot promise they remain fully accurate. What I can tell you honestly is that monitoring, on its own, is not the same as intervention. Forty million years of observation has not, as far as any record indicates, produced direct contact or interference of any kind.*

"Until now, potentially."

*Until now, potentially,* she agreed. *I do not know yet what the reactivation means. I know only that it is real, that you found it yourself before I told you, and that I owed you the full context the moment that became true.*

He nodded slowly, the weight of the disclosure settling into him alongside everything else this world had already asked him to carry — Veth-Karun's dissolution, the Pelagic Archive's sealed door, the dormant deep-space signal still waiting, unanswered, somewhere in the outer dark.

One more door, he understood, opening quietly into something considerably larger than anything he'd yet fully prepared himself for.

He found, sitting with it, that he wasn't ready to be afraid yet. He was, instead, already beginning, methodically and carefully, to think about what came next.
