# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Seven — What's Inside

*Scale Walking: Practical Techniques for the Compression Practitioner* was not a document written for beginners.

He had known this would be the case — the archive's access system had been calibrated to ensure that only practitioners who had developed sufficient capability could open the containers that held the more advanced material. He had opened this one by developing dual-scale awareness. The manual had been waiting behind that specific capability for a reason.

Still. He had expected more preamble.

The manual opened directly with a calibration table:

*Your current Compression ratio determines your operational scale. Cross-reference your ratio against the scale table to identify your current working context.*

The scale table ran from Ratio 1 (ambient/building scale, first-stage perception) through Ratio 7 (subatomic scale, direct lattice interaction). Seven ratios. He had been operating at Ratios 1 through 2 and thought this was the system's full range.

He had been working with two of seven levels.

---

He spent four hours with the scale table before reading anything else.

The implications built as he worked through them:

Ratio 1: ambient Qi perception, building-scale manipulation. *This is his baseline. Eight months of work has made this fluent.*

Ratio 2: molecular-scale perception, protein-level manipulation. *He had achieved this. The dual-scale lock operation had required it.*

Ratio 3: cellular-architecture scale, passage through non-dense matter. *He could not yet do this. This would require sustained Ratio 3 maintenance — different geometry from Ratio 2, harder to establish.*

Ratio 4: micro-scale, direct interaction with Chitin Circuit inscription geometry. *What the Formicans used for inscription work at the highest level of precision. What he was currently approximating using Ratio 1.7.*

Ratio 5: nano-scale, direct Veritonal substrate interaction. *The level at which the Under-Web's filament network could be directly modified rather than influenced.*

Ratio 6: quantum-scale, direct observation of the Veritonal substrate's quantum behavior. *The level described in Document 3's foundational physics sections.*

Ratio 7: subatomic, direct lattice interaction. *The Codex described this ratio in notation so dense he had not yet attempted to decode it. The manual's scale table noted, with characteristic Formican understatement: 'Ratio 7 operation requires sustained practice at Ratios 5 and 6 for a period measured in decades rather than months.'*

He had been operating at the first two levels of a seven-level system.

He held this.

Then he wrote:

> *Scale Walking manual, primary finding: 7 Compression ratios, not 2. I have been operating in the first 28% of the system's depth.*
>
> *This is not discouraging. This is information.*
>
> *What changes: everything about my training timeline. I have been on a pace toward 'complete Compression capability' that was actually pace toward 'adequate Ratio 2.' Complete capability is seven levels. I have two.*
>
> *What doesn't change: the method. Comprehension precedes capability. Work down through the scale table in order. Don't skip.*
>
> *Ratio 3 is the next target. The manual describes prerequisites: stable Ratio 2 sustained for 15+ minutes (current max: 8 minutes and improving), dual-scale awareness as a natural operating mode rather than an effortful technique (partially achieved: the lock operation), and — the most interesting requirement — a specific comprehension of what passage through matter means at the scale at which matter is mostly space.*
>
> *Passage through non-dense matter. At Ratio 3, my compressed interface can navigate through biological tissue, wood, standard construction materials. Not through dense metals or the Strata's chitin-composite. The Codex's geometry for this is in Document 7.*
>
> *Document 7 is still locked. The prerequisite for Document 7 is Ratio 3 capability.*
>
> *The comprehension lock always requires you to understand the next thing in order to access the materials that explain the next thing. The unlock is always demonstrated capability, not theoretical knowledge.*

He paused. Added:

> *This is a good system. If frustrating.*

He turned to the manual's first practical chapter and began reading.

The chapter was titled: *Stability, Duration, and the Problem of Re-expansion.*

He read for two more hours.

When he surfaced from the Strata that evening, he felt the way he felt after understanding something that reorganized a large category — not triumphant, not overwhelmed, but recalibrated. The map had changed scale. The territory was the same. He was standing in the same place but could now see further in every direction.

Seven levels.

He had two.

There was a great deal of work ahead.

He was not, he noticed, anything other than interested.
