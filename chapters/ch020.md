# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Twenty — The Second Message

The Codex changed at 3:08 AM on the twenty-fourth day.

Lin Mushi noticed it because he had checked his phone at 3:07 during the dead stretch — habit — and the diagram had been its usual self: amber-gold lines, slow movement, the familiar distribution of nodes and connecting paths that he had spent two weeks mapping and partially decoding. He looked up. A customer came in. He rang through a bottle of water. The customer left. He looked back down.

The diagram had a second layer.

---

Not a second interface. Not a new app or a notification or a menu that had been hiding somewhere. A second layer within the diagram itself — visible now as a separate stratum, sitting behind the original geometry the way a transparency sits behind the image projected onto it. Fainter. In a different color, if color was the right word: the original lines were amber-gold and warm, and the new layer was cooler, silver-adjacent, precise in a way the first layer was not.

He stared at it for a long time.

The new layer had no moving lines. It was static — a fixed structure, unlike the first layer's slow pulse. It had nodes of its own, positioned at different points from the first layer's nodes, some overlapping, some sitting in the spaces between. It looked like an annotation.

It looked like a second map overlaid on the first, drawn in a different hand.

He pressed nothing. He touched nothing. He just looked until his eyes were certain they were seeing what they were seeing and not constructing it from tiredness or expectation.

Then he looked at the secondary nodes more carefully.

One was bright.

---

The message appeared when he touched it.

Not a text layer like before — something different. A sequence, or a set of sequences, unfolding as he watched, the notation moving across the screen with the deliberate pace of something being written rather than displayed. As if the message was being composed in real time. As if Ren Wuji had anticipated this moment and built the delivery to feel present rather than recorded.

He could read it. His notation literacy had expanded enough in twenty-four days that he didn't need the strange unlocking experience of the first message — he simply read, with the effort of someone reading a language they're still learning but can follow with concentration:

*Day twenty-four. Approximately.*

*If you are reading this, the comprehension lock has opened. You have documented what you perceived, questioned what you documented, and applied what you understood. You have not rushed. This is the correct approach. It is also the only approach that works. The Chitin Seed cannot be forced. It can only be followed.*

*What you now perceive is the Under-Web: the Veritonal Energy network that underlies your planet's surface. You have begun to map it. Good. The map is the foundation of everything that follows.*

*This second message contains three things. First: the technical name for what you are doing, and the framework within which it is understood. Second: a warning about a consequence you have already created that you should be aware of. Third: the location of the next step.*

*I will begin with the warning, because the other things will be easier to receive once you have it.*

*By using the Under-Web's ambient energy — to sustain the Codex, and once to address a physical injury — you have created a depletion signature in the local node. Depletion signatures are visible to anyone with the appropriate perceptual training. There are several such people in your city. One in particular monitors this node on behalf of an organization with resources and authority. This person does not yet know the source of the depletion. They are looking.*

*You have approximately four months before they find you. Possibly longer. This depends on how carefully you proceed from here.*

*I am telling you this not to cause alarm but because you should understand the pace of your situation. Four months is adequate time. You are not in immediate danger. You should, however, begin working faster than you have been.*

*The technical framework: what you are engaging in is called Scale Walking. Its principles are as follows—*

The message continued.

---

Lin Mushi read it standing at the counter with the store empty around him, the fluorescent light humming, the filaments running their patient amber paths through the floor.

He read it through once for comprehension and once for detail and a third time for the things he'd missed on the first two passes, which was how he always read anything that mattered. He wrote nothing during the reading. He made notes after.

> *Second message received, Day 24, 3:08 AM.*
>
> *Contents:*
>
> *(1) Technical framework confirmed: the energy network = Under-Web. My engagement with it = Scale Walking. The practice has a name. The practice has a history. The Codex contains both.*
>
> *(2) Warning: depletion signature is visible. Northern Peak Court investigator (no name given) is monitoring the Chengdu node. Has noticed the anomaly. Does not yet know source. Timeline: four months approximate.*
>
> *(3) Next step location: given in notation I am 60% confident I've decoded correctly. Will verify.*
>
> *Processing order:*
>
> *Warning first. Four months. I have been operating as if I had unlimited time. I do not. This changes the pace but not the method — I still need to understand what I'm doing before I do more of it, but I need to understand faster.*
>
> *Framework second. 'Scale Walking' — Ren Wuji used this term and a set of principles I've noted separately. The first principle is: perception precedes practice. The second is: comprehension unlocks access. The third is: each scale of perception is a different mode of existence, not merely a different level of resolution.*
>
> *The third principle I don't fully understand yet. Filing.*
>
> *Next step third. Verify the location first. Approach after verification. Rule: map before entry.*

He set the pen down.

He looked at the diagram, both layers now, the amber-gold and the cooler silver, the Under-Web and the Codex's annotation layer sitting one behind the other.

He had been found by something that no one who mattered to him knew existed. He was being looked for by someone who also didn't know he existed. He had four months of anonymity, possibly more.

The store was quiet. A car passed outside. Somewhere a phone buzzed and went to voicemail.

He thought about what Ren Wuji had said in the first message: *I hope you are curious. Everything depends on it.*

He was curious. He was also, now, aware that everything depending on it was a statement with a specific weight, not a rhetorical flourish. The man had spent four hundred years on this, had found him in four minutes, had written him messages that unlocked themselves by comprehension rather than on a timer. Every decision in the transmission had been deliberate.

The timeline was deliberate too.

*You have approximately four months.*

He had earned this warning by working carefully. The warning was a gift.

He uncapped his pen again and wrote at the bottom of the page:

> *He trusted me with the timeline because he trusted me to use it well.*
>
> *Four months. I will use them well.*

He closed the notebook. He straightened the snack display that didn't need straightening. He served the 4 AM customer — a nurse from the hospital two blocks over who came in every few weeks and always bought the same juice and always looked profoundly tired — and said "goodnight" when she left.

At 5:50 AM, with ten minutes before Xiao Dong arrived, he opened the Codex to the notation he'd identified as the next step's location, and began the work of confirming whether he'd read it correctly.

He had.

He sat with that for a moment.

North-northwest. Deeper than the store. Deeper than the tributary.

The main current.

He had a map. He had a timeline. He had a warning he'd earned by paying attention.

He had four months to understand enough to deserve what came next.

He started a new page: *Scale Walking — Principles and Framework.*

This was the comprehension lock opening.

He had passed it by doing the work, and the work was just beginning.
