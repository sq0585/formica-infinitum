# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Sixty-Nine — The Chitin Seed Responds

He achieved stable Ratio 2 on a Sunday at 2:44 PM — not 2 AM, not 4 AM, but 2:44 PM on an ordinary Sunday afternoon when the light through the windows was the flat gray of Chengdu December and someone in the building across the alley was playing a piano badly, working through the same passage repeatedly.

He had been practicing for twelve days. He had the balance quality — inhabit, not hold — and he had applied it across forty-seven attempts of varying duration. At 2:44 PM he applied it for the forty-eighth time and the geometry held.

Three seconds. Ten seconds. Thirty seconds. He was at Ratio 2, stable, perceiving the internal architecture of the filament network with a clarity he hadn't previously experienced.

At sixty seconds he made the decision to come back to Ratio 1 deliberately rather than wait for the geometry to collapse on its own, because deliberate exit was a different skill from collapse-recovery and he needed both.

He exited.

He breathed.

He was fine.

---

The Chitin Seed responded at 3:02 PM.

He had been documenting the stable Compression in his notebook when he became aware that something was happening that was distinct from anything he'd consciously attempted. The Seed's warmth — the constant background of its integration — expanded suddenly, not in intensity but in scope: a quality of fullness, as if something that had been reaching its edge had been released to grow past it.

Seven channels opened.

Not one. Not sequentially. All seven, in the space of forty seconds, each arriving with a specific new quality of perception.

He sat very still and let it happen.

Secondary channels — the Codex's document 5, which had unlocked two days ago, described these as the *auxiliary network*, channels that operated on the twelve-channel primary system's foundation and extended its reach rather than adding new fundamental capability. They were not new pathways in the meridian architecture; they were refinements of the existing ones, like adjusting the resolution of a lens that was already correctly focused. Each one added resolution, range, or depth to a sense he already had.

The seventh and last deepened his awareness of his own channels themselves — a meta-perception, a sense of the interface geometry from the inside rather than from its effects. When it settled, the warmth in his body stopped being something he noticed and became something he simply was. The Seed had stopped being a foreign element that was integrating and had become part of the structure, indistinguishable in quality from the biological tissue around it.

He wrote:

> *2:44 PM. Stable Ratio 2 — first time. 60 seconds.*
>
> *3:02 PM. Chitin Seed growth phase. 7 auxiliary channels opening over 40 seconds. These are not additional primary channels — the twelve-channel circuit completed in Month 3. These are refinements: perception extensions, resolution upgrades, depth additions to each existing sense. The auxiliary network is to the primary channels what fine-grained calibration is to a well-built instrument.*
>
> *What changed: the warmth is now native. Not something in me — something I am. The Seed is not in my nervous system. The Seed is my nervous system's Veritonal layer.*
>
> *This is the comprehension lock reward for genuine Compression. Not the practice itself — the genuine achievement of stable Ratio 2.*
>
> *The Codex taught: comprehension unlocks capability. I have demonstrated this in every channel opening. This is the first time the capability unlocked was not a new perception but a new state of being.*
>
> *I don't have good language for what changed. The closest I have: I am now more of a single thing than I was.*

He sat with this for a while.

The piano across the alley was still working through the same passage. It was getting slightly better. The pianist had found something in the repetition.

He had been studying his own cultivation for five months and he had arrived at a state the Codex described but that he had not been able to imagine from description.

He thought about Ren Wuji's voice: *dry, precise, faintly amused.*

He thought: this is what four hundred years of this looks like, from the other end. Not the arrival but the continued working. The stable Ratio 2 was not a destination. It was the prerequisite for understanding the next section of the Codex.

There was always a next section.

He turned to Document 5.

He had a growth phase's worth of new capability and a node that was dead and an investigator who now had his name.

He had work.
