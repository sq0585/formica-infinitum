# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Sixty — Month Four

Four months.

He was aware of this as a fact rather than a milestone, because milestones implied ceremonies and he was not a person who found ceremonies useful. But the number was there: four months since the man who turned out to have been named Ren Wuji had died at a counter at 3:17 AM.

The fifth audio file arrived that morning, which he had been waiting for without knowing he was waiting — the Codex's notification pattern had become familiar enough that the distinctive chime registered as significant before he'd consciously identified it.

He sat down with his earphones.

Ren Wuji's voice:

"You are approaching the threshold. This file is about what you will find when you cross it.

"The Strata below Chengdu is not an abandoned ruin. I want to say this first and clearly, because the natural assumption — a person discovers an ancient underground structure, it is in some state of historical preservation — is wrong in a specific way that will matter immediately.

"The Strata is occupied. The Formican community in the deeper layers has been there continuously, not since I arrived, not since the Amber Lattice School operated, but for a period of time the historical records describe in notation I will leave for you to translate when your comprehension reaches that section. The community is substantial. They are aware of the surface world in more detail than you would currently assume. They have been aware of you for some weeks.

"They have chosen not to make contact until you crossed the threshold. This is protocol, not indifference.

"When you enter: you will find a vaulted hall. The geometry is hexagonal at every scale and you will recognize it from the Codex notation. The floor will illuminate under your feet — a recognition response, not a threat. The ambient Qi density is considerably higher than the surface, which will affect your perception range and your Compression capability. Both will increase.

"You will encounter a Keeper. The Keeper is an individual Formican assigned to the station's maintenance function. Their role is interface management — they are not the community's leadership, they are not the archive's authority, they are the person whose job it is to manage the relationship between the deeper layers and whatever arrives from above. They will observe you. They will not speak to you immediately. Give them time. They are also learning.

"The library is in the northern section of the hall. The containers in the outer library are accessible at your current capability level. The deeper sections require comprehension development you do not yet have. Work through the outer section methodically. There is more there than you will initially perceive.

"One practical warning: do not attempt to open any sealed container by force. The archive's access tiers are calibrated thresholds — pushing past them produces not entry but damage to the material inside. The containers know whether you are ready. Trust the system.

"The sixth file will unlock when you have spent three entries inside and have begun to understand the communication register. You'll know what I mean by then.

"Go. You have been waiting long enough."

---

He wrote the assessment at his desk with the notebook open and the Codex diagram running its patient amber breath on the phone beside it.

---

> *Month 4 assessment:*
>
> *Channels: 12 primary, all open, all in harmonic resonance. Interface geometry: stable at Ratio 1. Preliminary 1.5 achieved briefly in practice sessions.*
>
> *Codex progress: Documents 1, 2, 3 fully decoded. Document 4 (Philosophy of Scale) — 80%. Documents 5–7: unlocking in sequence as comprehension develops.*
>
> *Perception range: Under-Web filaments within 60 meters, continuous. Structural properties: 15-meter radius. EM fields: 15 meters. Atmospheric Qi texture: 8 meters. Emotional Qi field: 3–5 meters, direct contact improves resolution.*
>
> *Notation fluency: approximately 80% of encountered symbols readable without reference. Grammar: fluent for the documents decoded so far.*
>
> *Strata knowledge: cross-section schematic of 4 layers to 400m depth. Entry threshold located. Entry protocol understood theoretically.*
>
> *Node status: 8% capacity. Terminal. Collapse estimated 2–3 weeks.*
>
> *Financial: 10 weeks of savings remaining. 2 active geological consulting contracts. Income approximately 1,800 yuan/month, approaching sustainability threshold.*
>
> *Timeline: the investigator is in the city. The node collapse will trigger escalation. I have days, not weeks, before the situation requires a response.*

He looked at the financial line.

He had started the consulting because he needed income. What it had turned out to be was something more specific: the application of his Qi perception to problems that affected real infrastructure, real buildings, real people who would live in those buildings. He had found three subsurface voids in the past month that conventional survey methods had missed. One of them was under a planned residential building. The discovery meant foundation redesign, which cost money and time, and also meant the building wouldn't fail in five years when the void's ceiling collapsed.

He had not known about this consequence when he started consulting. He had been trying to pay rent.

He noted it now:

> *The consulting work: I am applying Qi perception to infrastructure problems. Real problems with real consequences. The Codex teaches scale as discipline — the correct intervention at the correct scale produces results that a mismatched intervention cannot. I have been doing this without fully knowing I was doing it.*
>
> *Three avoided structural failures in four weeks.*
>
> *This is what I meant, in chapter 34, when I wrote about what the knowledge is for. I did not know the specific answer then.*
>
> *This is one specific answer: applied to the problems in front of me, it helps.*

He closed the assessment.

He had ten weeks of money, twelve channels, and a threshold to cross in two days.

He had, for the first time in four months, something resembling a sense of direction.

He turned to the Compression geometry one last time before bed.

Wednesday. Before the node died. Before the investigator found him.

He had work to do.
