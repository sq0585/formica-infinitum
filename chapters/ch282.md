# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Two Hundred and Eighty-Two — Month Twenty-Two, Planetary Scale Awareness

He noticed the change gradually, the way most expansions of his perception had arrived — not a single dramatic threshold crossed, but an accumulation he only fully registered once he stopped to measure it properly.

The Colony Link's range had grown considerably since the consent protocol's early days, back when "everyone in the Strata" had felt like an overwhelming, barely manageable expansion. He sat down one evening, the careful, methodical habit of measurement he'd built across two years asserting itself, and extended his perception outward deliberately, mapping exactly how far it now reached.

Eighty kilometers, give or take.

The number startled him more than he expected. Eighty kilometers meant the entire Chengdu metropolitan area, every district, every neighborhood, the full sprawling weight of a city of over twenty million people, all of it now sitting somewhere within range of an awareness he hadn't deliberately built to reach this far.

He didn't perceive each person individually — that, he understood from the careful study he'd done of the mechanism, would have been impossible at this scale, the way a single drop of water in an ocean doesn't register as a distinct entity against the whole. What he felt instead was texture, a vast, shifting emotional weather system, the accumulated mood of a city's worth of people living their ordinary lives, layered and overlapping in ways he was still learning to read.

He wrote about it carefully that evening, the particular discipline of honest documentation he'd never let himself abandon, regardless of how strange or unsettling the subject matter became.

> *Colony Link range: approximately 80 km radius, confirmed through deliberate measurement. This covers the full Chengdu metropolitan area.*
>
> *I do not perceive individuals at this range. I perceive texture — collective mood, broad emotional weather, shifting through the day in patterns I'm only beginning to recognize. Morning carries a different quality than evening. Weekdays differ from weekends in ways I can feel before I consciously register the calendar.*
>
> *Some days I surface from a cultivation session and don't immediately know which feelings are mine and which are the city's. This is getting harder to manage, not easier, as the range continues to grow.*

He set the pen down, the honest admission sitting heavier than the careful, clinical language around it suggested.

It wasn't dramatic. He understood that clearly, sitting with it — there was no single crisis moment, no acute breakdown of the kind that might make for a compelling account if anyone were narrating his life as a story rather than simply living it. It was instead a slow, accumulating burden, the particular psychological cost of carrying awareness that had grown past any scale a human mind had originally been built to hold.

He thought about Chen Yufei, about Liang Jingzhi, about the two Anchors already helping to hold his coherence steady against an expansion that showed no signs of naturally stopping on its own.

He thought, with a particular unease he hadn't fully articulated even to himself yet, about what happened if the expansion continued indefinitely — past eighty kilometers, past the metropolitan area, past whatever boundary currently felt like the edge of what he could manage.

He didn't have an answer yet. He wrote the question down anyway, the same careful discipline that had served him through every previous unknown this world had handed him.

> *Question I don't yet have an answer to: does this expansion have a natural ceiling, or does it simply continue growing as my overall cultivation deepens? If the latter, I need to understand what happens at the far end of that trajectory before I arrive there unprepared.*

He closed the notebook, the evening settling around him with its familiar amber quiet, and found, sitting with the honest weight of an unanswered question, that he trusted the discipline of simply noting it clearly more than he trusted any comfortable reassurance he might have manufactured instead.

He would find the answer eventually. He always did. For tonight, managing the texture of twenty million ordinary lives settling quietly into the edge of his awareness, noting it honestly was enough.
