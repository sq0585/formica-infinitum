# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Seventy — The Investigator's Lead

Elder Kuo Tianming called his contact at the Northern Peak Court's administrative office at 8 PM on a Tuesday.

The call was brief, professional, and contained a request that he had been building toward for three weeks: a background inquiry on the name *Lin Mushi*, former night-shift employee of a 7-Eleven in Wuhou District, Chengdu, who had resigned approximately three months ago and had been present during what appeared to be the initial onset of the node depletion event.

He submitted the name. He waited.

The return call came four hours later.

---

What the background inquiry produced:

Name: Lin Mushi. Age 22. Former university student, dropout, no cultivation record on file with any of the five Courts or their affiliated registries. Employment: 7-Eleven night shift, eight months, resigned three months ago. Current occupation: self-described geological consultant, operating as a freelancer. No physical address on file beyond his registered residence in Wuhou District.

Also: three recent geological survey reports, available through the professional registry where he'd filed his credentials. Accurate. Specifically, startlingly accurate — structural void locations, water table anomalies, subsurface irregularities that standard survey methods would have missed entirely.

Kuo read the survey reports twice.

He wrote in his field notes: *The source of the node depletion is almost certainly an unregistered, self-trained cultivator operating at an unusual level of technical sophistication. The geological surveys demonstrate Qi-based spatial perception at a range and resolution that exceeds standard Micro Scale capability for a practitioner of comparable apparent experience.*

He paused. He added: *The signature is not conventional. Self-taught, technically strange, and surprisingly sophisticated.* He had written this before, but it needed updating: *More sophisticated than previously assessed. Request backup and authorization for enhanced acquisition protocol.*

He submitted the request.

He then spent an hour looking at the survey reports again.

The accurate location of a subsurface void at six meters depth, with precise dimensional estimates, provided through what was described as "tactile assessment methods." The identification of a seasonal water table incursion from surface indicators. The correct prediction of a foundation failure risk three weeks before the standard survey confirmed it.

This was not what an unregistered practitioner typically did with Qi perception. Unregistered practitioners typically used their capability for self-cultivation, for minor advantages, for the usual catalogue of things people did when they had an edge others didn't.

This practitioner was solving engineering problems.

Kuo did not know what to do with this. It didn't fit the category he'd been using.

He noted it and filed it and waited for the authorization.

The authorization came the following morning: enhanced acquisition protocol approved, backup practitioner in transit, two weeks.

Two weeks.

He had Lin Mushi's name, his address, his professional history.

He would have him within two weeks.

---

Lin Mushi did not know any of this.

What he knew was that the node was dead and the situation was critical and he had been inside the Strata for six entries now and had still not made contact with the presence to the north, which he had begun to think of as patient rather than avoidant — something that was waiting for something from him rather than avoiding him entirely.

He was going in again tomorrow.

He was going to try to approach the presence.

He was going to demonstrate what he was before someone else got to define it for him.
