# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Thirty-Eight — The Colony's Edge

At Ratio 4, the Colony Link was not a thing he perceived.

It was the thing he perceived within.

He had been developing this understanding across six attempts over two weeks, each one adding a minute or two to his duration, each one giving him more time to work with the filtering technique that Document 7 had described and that the manual's Ratio 4 section had elaborated. The filtering was the key: without it, Ratio 4 was overwhelming. With it, the information remained present but navigable — the way driving in a busy city was different from standing in the middle of an intersection and trying to track every vehicle simultaneously.

On the seventh attempt, he sat in the library.

Not the corridor. He had moved to the library specifically because the library's ambient Qi density was higher and more organized than the corridor's, and because the Colony Link field there had a different quality — shaped by the archive's maintenance circuits rather than just the raw Strata structure.

He reached Ratio 4 and activated the filtering.

And then, for the first time at Ratio 4, he listened.

---

The Formican community in Layer 3 was not audible. He had no word for what it was, actually, except that it was present in the way that something enormous and close is present — not as a sensation exactly, not as sound, but as the quality of the environment having a direction.

He held himself still and attended.

At Ratio 1 the Colony Link field of the community below appeared as a warm general presence, like the warmth of a building that is occupied from the outside. At Ratio 4, the field did not appear at all in the sense of appearing. It was what was there, and he was in it, and the community's collective quality was the quality of the medium he existed in.

He attended to the medium.

What was in it: not individual presences, not individual minds, not anything that resolved into discrete signals. A collective state, organized the way a body's nervous system is organized — not centralized in any one place, distributed through every part, information flowing through the distribution in patterns that constituted the collective's ongoing processing.

The collective was working.

He could feel, without being able to read the specific content, that the work was something like: monitoring, assessing, deciding. The quality of a community that has received new information and is processing its response.

The document. They knew about the document. The alert propagated through the Colony Link field the way a vibration propagated through material — not transmitted as a message, distributed as a state change.

Two hundred Formicans below Chengdu, knowing that the surface had received the translation document.

He sat in the library at Ratio 4 for exactly twelve minutes — his new stable duration — and listened to the field.

He did not understand the specific content. He was at the early stages of Colony Stage perception, the individual-reading stage extending toward the field-reading stage. The field was there; the content was not yet clear.

But the quality was.

They were not alarmed.

He had expected some version of alarm — a human surface-dweller releasing documents about the nature of the Veritonal system was, from the Formican community's perspective, a potentially significant change in the surface world's relationship to the Under-Web. Whether that was a threat or a development depended on what the community valued.

The quality he was reading was closer to *interested* than *alarmed.*

He exited Ratio 4 at twelve minutes and two seconds, cleanly, following the geometric re-expansion sequence he'd developed.

He sat in the library and wrote:

> *Ratio 4, 7th attempt, 12 minutes 2 seconds — new stable duration. Filtering: operational.*
>
> *Colony Link at Ratio 4: not a perceived field. The medium itself. The community's collective state is the Strata's environmental character.*
>
> *Community response to document release: interested, not alarmed. I cannot read the content. The quality is clear.*
>
> *What this implies: they were watching what I was doing before I did it. They have context. The document is not a surprise.*
>
> *Axis-7 knew I would release it. She told me about the archive worker transferring Volume 1 'without consulting her.' She has been navigating between the archive's protocol and the community's collective processing for 180 years. The document may have been something the community has been anticipating for a long time.*
>
> *I should ask Axis-7 about this. Directly.*
>
> *Tomorrow.*

He went home.

He had reached twelve minutes at Ratio 4.

He needed twenty for the restricted section's lock.

Eight minutes to build.

The work continued.
