# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Twenty-Four — Thirty Days

One month.

He wrote the date at the top of a fresh page and sat looking at it.

One month since a man he didn't know had died in front of him at 3:17 AM on a Tuesday in October. One month since a hexagonal icon had appeared on his phone. One month since he had written, in his blue-covered notebook: *3:17 AM. Old man, no ID, entered without triggering door chime. Died standing. Before — hand movement, both hands, lasted less than half a second. Pattern I can't describe.*

He had described it now. He had named the pattern, mapped its mechanism, begun to understand its consequences. He had twenty-four days of documented Qi observations, a partial spatial survey of the store, eleven weeks until the timeline expired, a notation system forty percent decoded, and a structured warmth in his hands that could feel the structural properties of walls.

He made a list. Not of what he felt about any of this. Of what he knew.

---

> *Thirty-day assessment:*
>
> *What I can do:*
> *— Perceive the Under-Web (Qi filament network) spatially within approximately 50-meter radius*
> *— Detect structural properties of physical objects through direct contact*
> *— Direct ambient Qi to a specific location in my body (verified: wrist healing)*
> *— Perform minor environmental Qi redirection (verified: cold room)*
> *— Sense the general Qi state of people within close range*
> *— Partially read Formican notation (40%, increasing)*
>
> *What I know:*
> *— There is an energy network beneath observable reality (Under-Web / Veritonal Energy)*
> *— This network has been mapped and described by a civilization called Formican*
> *— He spent 400 years learning to navigate it and transmitted what he knew to me*
> *— There is a hidden community of human practitioners in this city who can partially perceive the network using traditional methods*
> *— One of them is monitoring the node I have been depleting*
> *— I have approximately 11 weeks before this monitoring leads to discovery*
> *— Beneath the store, there is a structure that is not geological. It has the geometry of architecture.*
>
> *What I don't know:*
> *— Almost everything.*
>
> *What I have:*
> *Six months ago, this list would have been terrifying.*
> *Now it seems like enough to start.*

He capped the pen.

---

He made himself a proper meal for the first time in three days — rice, egg, the greens his mother had pressed on him at his last visit, still usable. He ate at the desk with his notebooks pushed aside, not reading anything, just eating.

The warmth in his hands was a background constant now. He barely noticed it anymore except in specific moments of attention, the way you stop noticing a room's temperature once you've adjusted to it.

He thought about frameworks.

The second message had introduced *Scale Walking* as the technical framework. It had given him three principles. It had told him the Codex contained the full system. He had been working with the Codex through documentation and patient analysis, the only method he knew, and the method was working — comprehension accumulating slowly, each piece enabling the next.

But the second message had also said: *begin working faster than you have been.*

Faster did not mean careless. It meant he needed to identify what was blocking comprehension and remove the blocks before they became the timeline's limitation.

The blocks were:

First, notation. He was at forty percent. He needed to push past fifty because the documents beyond the first two were locked behind a comprehension threshold he could feel but not yet cross.

Second, spatial understanding. He could see the Under-Web but he had not yet been below the store. He was mapping the surface of something from the outside.

Third, whatever Scale Walking actually required physically. The second message had described it but he hadn't yet tried it deliberately. He had been perceiving; he had not yet been *walking.*

He washed his bowl, put it away, and opened the notation notebook.

*I have no framework for this. I will build one.*

That was still true. He was still building. The building was the work.

He had eleven weeks, and he was going to use them.
