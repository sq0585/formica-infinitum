# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter 482 — Six Weeks — The Process

He kept the record the way he kept every record that mattered — plainly, in structured entries, resisting the urge to dress six weeks of careful, patient work into more drama than it actually contained.

*Week One,* he wrote, once the first seven days had passed. *The inner boundary is established. The distributed network now extends fully from Earth's own Colony Link through the near stellar neighborhood, three hundred and twenty-three anchor points holding steady, no withdrawals, no coherence failures. Dr. Shen Yue's instruments confirm what I can feel directly: the structure is sound. This is the easiest week the process will offer, and I am trying not to let that ease breed false confidence about what remains ahead.*

The second week tested that caution almost immediately. The network's extension began, for the first time, to actually touch the dissolution zone's own outer edge, and the sensation, relayed back through three hundred and twenty-three individual points of contact, arrived considerably stranger than anything the preview sessions had prepared anyone for.

*Week Two,* he wrote. *Contact with the zone's outer boundary confirmed. Several anchor points report disorientation — not danger, Dr. Shen Yue's monitoring confirms, but the specific, difficult sensation of touching something that reads simultaneously as absence and as scale. Liang Jingzhi, positioned as our primary contact point given her geological training's unusual compatibility with reading structural entropy, describes it best: "It is like touching a room I know is empty and feeling, anyway, that something enormous used to be standing in it."*

*Week Three,* he wrote, several days later, watching Dr. Shen Yue's coherence graphs with the particular attention he reserved for data that might, at any moment, reveal a problem he needed to address quickly. *The Lattice's own self-repair function has begun, faintly, to respond. Not dramatically — a slow, patient shift in the residual structure's own behavior, the specific sensation of a much larger system finally, tentatively, recognizing our network as something worth responding to rather than simply another inert presence at its edge.*

*Week Four,* the entry continued, the pattern of the process settling into something he was beginning to trust rather than merely hope for. *The response has strengthened. The Lattice's self-repair function now treats our extended Colony Link consistently as a valid reference pattern — exactly the theoretical outcome Veth-Karun's account, indirectly, promised was possible. I want to be careful not to overstate this. The zone remains vast, the damage remains extensive, and four weeks of progress against forty million years of accumulated entropy is, by any honest measure, still an early beginning. But it is, unmistakably, a beginning that is working.*

Week five brought the first measurable evidence he could show the wider Collective, rather than simply describe.

*Week Five,* he wrote, and found himself, for once, allowing a small measure of genuine satisfaction into the entry's otherwise careful tone. *Dr. Shen Yue's instruments register measurable coherence improvement across the contacted region of the dissolution zone — a small percentage, against an enormous total scale, but real, verified independently, not merely felt by those of us extended into the structure directly. I transmitted the data to the Surveyor this morning. Its response consisted of a single line: "This is the first measurable Formless Scale repair progress in Collective recorded history." I am choosing not to let that sentence go to my head. I am, admittedly, finding it somewhat difficult.*

The sixth week arrived, finally, with the outcome the entire six-week structure had been quietly building toward.

*Week Six,* he wrote, and found the entry, when he finally set it down, carried a weight he hadn't fully anticipated feeling. *The process has become self-sustaining. The Lattice's own self-repair function now maintains the established reference pattern's momentum without requiring my continued active extension. This does not mean the work is finished — Dr. Shen Yue estimates, cautiously, that full repair remains a project measured in years rather than weeks. But it means the foundation is genuinely, structurally sound, and it means, for the first time since this began, that I can step back from active maintenance without the entire effort collapsing behind me.*

*Liang Jingzhi's own extension settled into place this same week, quietly, without ceremony, alongside everything else finally holding on its own. Meen-4 confirms the permanent node is stable. Liang Jingzhi herself, when I asked how it felt to have it finished, said only: "It isn't finished. It's just no longer temporary." I am still turning that sentence over.*

He closed the entry, and sat for a long moment with the specific, quiet relief of six weeks' careful, disciplined work finally, honestly, holding.
