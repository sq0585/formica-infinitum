# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter 408 — The Colony Link — After

In the weeks that followed, he found himself paying closer attention to something he had, for years, simply taken for granted — the specific, familiar texture of Chen Yufei's presence at the edge of his awareness, always there, woven into the Colony Link's ambient structure the way certain sounds become invisible to a person who has lived beside them long enough.

She was still present, exactly as she'd promised. He sensed her the way he always had — a steady signature among the twelve hundred others the Link carried, distinct in the same particular way it had always been distinct, unmistakably hers among a crowd of unfamiliar signals. The Anchor bond had not thinned. If anything, in the days immediately following the separation, he found himself more aware of its steadiness than he had been in months, perhaps because some part of him had, without quite admitting it, feared that ending the romantic relationship might somehow bleed into the bond itself, weakening something that had never been conditional on the other.

It hadn't. The distinction she'd insisted on, that first night, held exactly as firmly as she'd promised it would.

What had changed, subtly, was how he chose to hold his own attention around her presence.

He noticed it first about ten days in — a moment of ordinary awareness, checking in on the Strata's ambient emotional field the way he did dozens of times a day, and finding his attention drifting, briefly, toward the specific texture of her signature longer than the check actually required. Not intrusive. Not a violation of anything she'd have objected to. Just a lingering, the kind of unconscious attention a person gives to something they're used to holding close, even after the reason for holding it closely has changed shape.

He pulled back, deliberately, and kept doing it in the days that followed — not because she asked him to, she never once asked, and he suspected she would have told him plainly if the ordinary functioning of the Anchor bond bothered her in any way. He filtered his attention more carefully simply because it felt, on reflection, like the right thing to do. Not doing so would have been easy. It would also, he understood, have been a small, quiet dishonesty toward the new shape of what they'd agreed to become — treating the bond's continued closeness as license to keep something of the old intimacy alive by default, rather than letting the relationship actually become what they'd both said, honestly, they wanted it to become.

"You've gotten quieter, at the edge of things," Chen Yufei observed, about three weeks in, during one of their now-regular Anchor maintenance sessions — a formal, scheduled practice they'd both agreed made sense to establish, now that the bond's care could no longer rely on the casual proximity of daily romantic life.

"I noticed I was lingering more than I needed to," he admitted. "Checking in on you longer than an actual check required. I didn't think you'd asked me to stop. I just thought it was the right thing to do anyway."

She was quiet a moment, something moving behind her expression that he couldn't fully read. "I hadn't asked," she confirmed. "And I want you to know, I wouldn't have minded, particularly, if you hadn't noticed on your own. But I appreciate that you did. It tells me something about how seriously you're taking the distinction we drew, even in the small, private moments no one else would ever know about."

"I meant what I said," he told her. "About the Anchor not being conditional on the rest of it. I wanted to actually live that, not just say it once and let the old habits creep back in unexamined."

"I believe you," she said, and something in her voice carried real warmth, the particular ease of two people who had navigated a genuinely difficult change and found, on the other side of it, that the foundation underneath it had held exactly as steady as they'd both hoped.

They finished the session in comfortable quiet, the bond humming along exactly as it always had — present, steady, unmistakably hers, and entirely, deliberately, no longer anything more than that.
