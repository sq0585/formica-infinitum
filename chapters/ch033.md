# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Thirty-Three — Second Experiment

The second meridian opening had been the *shaoyin* — the little finger channel, EM sensitivity.

He spent five days verifying this through the TCM texts' descriptions of what shaoyin activation felt like, what it was supposed to affect, and where along the arm the sensation had traveled. The descriptions were imprecise — "warmth descending from the heart to the fifth finger" was accurate enough to be meaningful and vague enough to be frustrating — but they were consistent with his experience, and consistency was what he had.

He also verified, by a different method: the Codex's Standard Protocol included a check for the first two channel openings, described in the rhythm notation as a *resonance confirmation*. The resonance confirmation involved sustaining both channels simultaneously — holding the north-northwest directional attention while also holding awareness of the EM-sensitive pathway — and observing whether the two created interference or harmonic reinforcement.

Harmonic reinforcement meant both were open and aligned. Interference meant something was wrong.

He ran the confirmation at 2 AM on a Thursday night, in the stockroom again, with the spare till rolls looking on with their usual neutrality.

Harmonic reinforcement. Both channels.

He wrote: *Confirmed. 2 channels open, verified resonance alignment.*

Then he started on the third.

---

The third channel, by the Standard Protocol's sequencing, was the *taiyin* lung channel — his original instinct about the warmth in his hands had been right weeks ago, it ran from the chest through the inner arm to the thumb, and it was the channel most directly connected to breath regulation and perception of atmospheric Qi.

He had been using it already, in the sense of applying the breath regulation from Phase Four. Opening it formally, following the Protocol's sequencing, would stabilize what was currently an unpracticed instinct into something he could rely on.

He attempted it carefully, following the mapping he'd developed: attention to the chest point, breath regulation, the four-two-six-two cadence, and then a specific directed attention moving down his left arm toward his thumb.

At the twenty-two minute mark, the channel opened.

He felt it as a line of clarity — the warmth running the specific anatomical path with a precision that confirmed the map. His thumb felt briefly warm and then normalized.

And then the world became slightly more detailed.

Not visually. Atmospherically. The air pressure in the stockroom arrived at him with a new granularity — not just direction and rough magnitude, but something closer to texture. He could tell the difference between the air near the metal shelving unit (slightly denser, colder, charged with the static of the metal's surface) and the air near the cardboard stock boxes (warmer, slightly humid, a different quality of Qi charge).

He sat with this for a long time, just breathing, learning what the new channel showed him.

Standard Protocol indicates twelve channels total as the complete Micro Scale interface — ten primary meridians plus the two governing vessels that form the full circulatory circuit. Nine more to go before the architecture is whole. The Codex specifies that seven open channels is the minimum for safe threshold entry; Compression prerequisites require the complete twelve.*
>
> *Revised timeline: I need to open channels faster. The node is depleting. I have eight weeks.*

He paused over the last line.

He had been systematic. He had been careful. He had been correct to be systematic and careful — the wrong approach to the first opening could have had consequences he couldn't yet fully assess.

But the situation had a clock and the clock did not care about his preference for thorough preparation.

Nine more channels. Eight weeks.

He could do more than one per week.

He would need to.

He closed the notebook and sat in the stockroom for a moment longer, feeling the atmospheric texture of the room in a way he hadn't been able to feel yesterday, the Chengdu October night coming through the walls as a kind of detailed information rather than just absence of warmth.

Then he went back to the counter because someone was coming through the door, and there was work to do.
