# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Eight — Scale Walking — Theory

He spent a week with the manual before attempting anything new.

This was discipline, not caution. The distinction mattered to him: caution was fear-based, a slowing-down because something might go wrong. Discipline was procedure-based, a deliberate choice to understand the theory fully before testing it, because tests run on incomplete theory produced results you couldn't interpret correctly. He had learned this in his first month, when the early channel openings had produced effects he couldn't assess because he didn't yet have the framework to situate them.

He had the framework now.

He was making sure it was the right framework before trusting it at Ratio 3.

---

*Scale Walking: Practical Techniques* organized its material around a central principle that the Codex had described philosophically but had not previously stated in operational terms:

*Each compression ratio is not a deeper version of the same perception. It is a different mode of existence. At Ratio 1, you are a building-scale being. At Ratio 2, you are a molecular-scale being. The practitioner does not perceive more of the same world at finer resolution. The practitioner inhabits a qualitatively different world in which the previous world's objects appear as vast, coarse structures rather than the environment.*

He had partially experienced this at Ratio 2: the floor of his apartment had become molecular architecture, the protein channels in his hands visible as luminous pathways. But he had interpreted this as *enhanced perception of the same environment* rather than what the manual described: a *different environment* in which his ordinary environment was background.

The distinction was not semantic.

At Ratio 3, the manual explained, the practitioner's interface was small enough that the spaces between molecules — which, at ordinary scale, were effectively nonexistent — became traversable. Solid matter was not solid. It was a statistical distribution of probability waves with large amounts of empty space between them. At Ratio 3, a practitioner could navigate through non-dense matter by passing through that space.

This was not magic. This was quantum mechanics, applied at the scale of cultivation technique.

He spent two days building from his existing physics self-education toward the relevant level of understanding. He read about wave-particle duality, about the probabilistic nature of matter, about the specific atomic orbital models that explained why solid objects felt solid at ordinary scale despite being mostly empty at sub-atomic scale.

Then he re-read the manual's Ratio 3 section with this framework in place.

The technique was now legible.

Not executable yet — he had the theory but not the stable Ratio 2 duration that served as prerequisite. But legible. He could see what the manual was describing. He could map it onto the physics. He could understand what the required geometry was doing.

He wrote:

> *Scale Walking theory — key revision:*
>
> *Previous model: Compression = enhanced perception at reduced scale.*
> *Corrected model: Compression = transition between qualitatively different modes of existence, each with its own physics and its own relationship to the ordinary world.*
>
> *At Ratio 1: I am a human. The world is human-scale.*
> *At Ratio 2: I am a molecule-scale being. Humans and buildings are vast, coarse structures around me.*
> *At Ratio 3: I am sub-cellular. Matter is a probabilistic distribution. Non-dense solid objects are traversable.*
>
> *The Formicans call this 'walking between scales' because it is, literally, that. Not seeing more. Being different. Temporarily.*
>
> *This changes the emotional architecture of the cultivation path. I have not been learning to perceive more. I have been learning to be something different, temporarily, deliberately, with the ability to return.*
>
> *The 'return' is the technique everyone underestimates. The manual devotes two full chapters to re-expansion because it is where practitioners fail.*
>
> *The Strata is not a pocket dimension in the sense of a separate space. It is a region of the ordinary world that is only accessible at specific compression ratios. It exists in ordinary space. It exists between the atoms.*

He read this back. He added:

> *The Formicans don't live underground. They live in the spaces between.*
>
> *This makes sense of everything.*

He closed the notebook.

He went to sleep.

He dreamed of something he couldn't describe afterward — not the amber quality of the early Codex dreams but something vast and geometrically consistent, a world that was ordinary Chengdu seen from a scale at which the city's buildings were geological features and the people in it were warm, moving points of probability.

He woke at 6 AM and started his Ratio 2 duration training.

He needed to reach fifteen minutes.

He was at eight.

He had seven minutes to build.
