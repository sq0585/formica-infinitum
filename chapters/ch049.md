# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Forty-Nine — Six More Channels

They opened in three days.

Not because he pushed them. Because he understood the architecture now, and understanding the architecture made each remaining channel obvious in a way the earlier ones hadn't been — the way a map makes a path obvious that had been invisible without it. He could see where each channel ran. He could see how it fit into the geometric whole. He could open them the way you'd answer a question once you'd understood what the question was asking.

Channel twelve on Monday evening: the *du* channel, the governing vessel, running up the spine and over the crown of the skull. It opened like a door being unlocked from the inside. He felt it travel its full length in one continuous warming, not the point-by-point progression of the earlier channels, and when it settled the geometry in his body closed into a complete circuit for the first time. Everything connected. Everything running.

He sat on the floor of his apartment for twenty minutes just perceiving what twelve channels in full harmonic felt like.

It felt like being made of something other than just himself.

---

Channel thirteen on Tuesday, before noon: a secondary channel, the *ren* vessel, running along the centerline of the front of the body. The complement to the twelfth. He had been warned in the notation that these two should ideally open within twenty-four hours of each other, which he'd interpreted as a timing recommendation and now understood as a structural requirement — the back and front meridians were a single circulatory system for the interface geometry, and opening one without the other was like completing half a circuit.

When thirteen opened, six hours after twelve, the interface felt stable in a way it had not before. Not just running — balanced.

---

Channels fourteen and fifteen opened together on Wednesday, which was not in the Protocol's sequencing but which the Protocol's notation had flagged as a *coherence event* — a spontaneous parallel activation that could occur when the core circuit was established and the remaining channels resolved the geometry simultaneously. He had read about this and classified it as aspirational. It was not aspirational. He was lying on his bed after the Thursday afternoon nap he'd been taking to compensate for three months of poor sleep when both opened without any effort at all, a warmth spreading through both arms simultaneously, the last geometric positions of the interface falling into place.

He lay still and counted: fourteen, fifteen.

Twelve plus thirteen plus fourteen plus fifteen.

He had all primary channels.

---

The third audio file unlocked at 4:03 PM on Wednesday, eleven minutes after channel fifteen completed.

The notification was quieter than the previous two — not the abrupt mid-practice buzz of file two, but a brief chime that he'd been told by the notation to expect. *When the primary circuit closes, the third record will open.* He had decoded that line three weeks ago and set it in the back of his attention the way you set a pot to simmer: present, attended to, not urgent until suddenly it was.

He sat down and put in his earphones.

Ren Wuji's voice:

"Twelve. You have the complete primary circuit.

"This file is practical. The earlier files were for understanding. This one is for what happens in your body over the next several weeks, so you know the difference between what is correct and what is alarming.

"First: the Chitin Seed will enter an integration phase. You will feel it as a change in the baseline warmth you've been experiencing — not a surge, not heat, but an expansion. The Seed's connection to your nervous system will extend into new territory. You'll feel it in your fingers first, then your hands, then settling somewhere you don't have good language for. This is correct. The Seed is building something in the biological substrate that the twelve channels give it permission to build. It was waiting for the circuit to close. Let it happen.

"Your perception range will increase as a side effect of this. I note this so you are not alarmed: suddenly being able to perceive things at sixty meters when yesterday you could only manage forty is not a sign that something went wrong. It is a sign that something went right.

"Second: you will now be able to read Document Three. Or to re-read it, if you've already attempted it, with comprehension you didn't have before. The Compression technique section specifically. You've seen the words. Now you'll understand what they mean. This is a reliable effect — the primary circuit's completion consistently unlocks this particular comprehension threshold.

"Third: what happens after this is harder to predict precisely, because it depends on you. The Compression practice ahead is not like the channel-opening practice. Channel-opening had clear external markers — a channel either opened or it didn't. Compression is more ambiguous, and the ambiguity is deliberate. You will need to develop your own internal calibration for when you are at ratio and when you only think you are.

"There is a quality to genuine Compression that is distinct from the sensation of attempting it. You will learn to tell the difference. You will also fail at this several times before you learn to tell the difference. This is fine. The failure modes are uncomfortable and not dangerous, which is exactly the right distribution for a learning process.

"One observation that falls between practical and personal: you have just done something that took me seven years. You have done it in three months. I do not say this as praise — I am aware that I am dead and that praise from me would be strange. I say it because it is an accurate measurement, and accurate measurements matter, and because I wanted you to know that I know.

"The fourth file unlocks at the Compression philosophy threshold. You will know when it happens.

"Keep working."

---

The Chitin Seed entered its growth phase at 4 PM on Wednesday.

He recognized it immediately from what Ren Wuji had just described — a distinct change in the baseline warmth, not a surge but an expansion, the Seed's integration extending into new territory with the confidence of a process that had been waiting for exactly this moment. He felt it in his fingertips first, then moving through his hands and up his forearms, then settling into something he didn't have good language for: a depth to his own physical presence that hadn't been there before, as if he had more interior volume.

He documented this carefully. He noted the time, the quality, the progression.

He noted also: his perception range had increased without deliberate effort. He could now feel the Under-Web's filament network at a radius of sixty meters, which was twenty meters more than his previous maximum. The resolution was higher too — the individual filaments distinguishable from each other at distances where they had previously blurred.

He could see the geometry of the network in more detail. Because he now understood his own interface's geometry in more detail.

The comprehension and the capability were not sequential. They were the same thing.

He sat at his desk and wrote, in large characters, in the center of a blank page:

> *Twelve primary channels: complete.*
>
> *The interface is built.*
>
> *Now I learn to use it.*

He held this for a moment.

Then he opened Document 3 to the Compression technique section that had been blocking him for weeks, and read it.

He understood every word.
