# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Forty-Nine — The Last Two Channels

They opened in two days.

Not because he pushed them. Because he understood the architecture now, and understanding the architecture made each remaining channel obvious in a way the earlier ones hadn't been — the way a map makes a path obvious that had been invisible without it. He could see where each channel ran. He could see how it fit into the geometric whole. He could open them the way you'd answer a question once you'd understood what the question was asking.

Channel eleven on Monday evening: the *du* channel, the governing vessel, running up the spine and over the crown of the skull. It opened like a door being unlocked from the inside. He felt it travel its full length in one continuous warming, not the point-by-point progression of the earlier channels, and when it settled the geometry in his body changed. Not in a way he could have predicted from the description. In a way that felt like a circuit finally closing — like a long-open loop completing itself through a connection he hadn't known was missing.

Everything ran differently. Not more strongly. More coherently.

He sat on the floor of his apartment for twenty minutes just perceiving what eleven channels in unified flow felt like.

It felt like being made of something other than just himself.

---

Channel twelve on Tuesday, before noon: the *ren* vessel, running along the centerline of the front of the body. The complement to the eleventh. He had been warned in the notation that these two should ideally open within twenty-four hours of each other, which he'd interpreted as a timing recommendation and now understood as a structural requirement — the governing vessels were a single circulatory system for the interface geometry, and opening one without the other was like completing half a circuit.

When twelve opened, six hours after eleven, the interface felt stable in a way it had not before. Not just running — balanced. The du running up the back, the ren completing the loop down the front, both governing the flow through the ten primary channels. One system. Twelve components. A whole.

He lay on the floor and counted, quietly, to himself: one through twelve.

He had all twelve channels.

---

The Chitin Seed entered a growth phase at 4 PM on Tuesday.

He recognized it from Ren Wuji's description in the third audio file — a distinct change in the baseline warmth, not a surge but an expansion, the Seed's integration into his nervous system extending into new territory with the confidence of a process that had been waiting for this moment. He felt it in his fingertips first, then moving through his hands and up his forearms, then settling into something he didn't have good language for: a depth to his own physical presence that hadn't been there before, as if he had more interior volume.

He documented this carefully. He noted the time, the quality, the progression.

He noted also: his perception range had increased without deliberate effort. He could now feel the Under-Web's filament network at a radius of sixty meters, which was twenty meters more than his previous maximum. The resolution was higher too — the individual filaments distinguishable from each other at distances where they had previously blurred.

---

He wrote in the observation notebook at 6 PM, after the growth phase had stabilized:

> *All twelve channels open and in unified harmonic. Circuit complete: ten primary pathways plus du and ren governing vessels.*
>
> *The governing vessels are not the twelfth and eleventh additions. They are the completion of a circuit that the ten primary channels were always part of. Opening them didn't add two new capabilities. It made the existing ten capabilities whole.*
>
> *Chitin Seed growth phase: ongoing. Perception range expanded to 60 meters. Resolution increased.*
>
> *Compression prerequisites: met. I can now attempt stable Ratio 1.5. The threshold crossing I completed at 9 channels was a preliminary entry. What I can do now is a different order of engagement.*
>
> *The Strata is waiting. The investigator has two weeks until backup arrives. The node is at approximately 15% of Day 17 baseline.*
>
> *I am ready.*

He closed the notebook.

He made tea. He ate properly for the first time in two days, rice and vegetables and something from the freezer that he reheated without paying attention to what it was.

Then he opened the Compression document and began to prepare.

Tomorrow he would enter the Strata with all twelve channels for the first time.

He wanted to know what that felt like.
