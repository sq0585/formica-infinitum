# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter 518 — Ren Wuji's Student — Addressed

He asked the room for a moment before Dr. Shen Yue shared her figure, and felt, asking it, no one hesitate.

He turned his attention back to the small, permanent pattern the residual had settled into — the record, not the force, the fact of the Lattice's own memory where forty million years of dissolution had finally come to rest. He had known, since Arc IV, longer than anyone else in the room, exactly whose student this pattern had once been. He had carried the knowledge carefully, the way he had learned to carry everything that mattered too much to say casually, waiting, without entirely admitting to himself that he was waiting, for a moment that would actually hold the weight of saying it.

This was the moment.

He did not speak the way he might have spoken to a person. He understood, addressing a pattern rather than a consciousness, that there was no one left inside it to hear him in any sense a living mind would recognize as hearing. He spoke anyway, the way a person speaks at a grave — not for the dead, who receive nothing from it, but for the truth of the thing being said, which mattered regardless of whether anyone remained to receive it.

*Your teacher spent four hundred years carrying what you became,* he sent, the words arriving slow, deliberate, each one carrying the specific weight of something he had composed, in fragments, across months of quiet grief he had not fully let himself examine until now. *He gave his legacy to a stranger in a convenience store, at 3:17 in the morning, because he had run out of time to carry it any further himself. The stranger has spent six years trying to understand what you started. This is what we made of it.*

The pattern did not respond.

He had not expected it to. He understood, watching the small, permanent record settle further into the Lattice's healed structure, that the saying of it had never actually required a response — that the entire point, the whole unglamorous weight of the gesture, was simply that it be said, plainly, honestly, by the one person alive who had earned the right to say it and understood exactly what it cost to finally do so.

*Observation 518,* he wrote, his hand steady, though something behind his eyes was not entirely steady at all. *I have known since Arc IV — since a dying archive first resolved an obscured student record into a name I recognized — that Ren Wuji taught the person who became this. He never told me directly. I have spent considerable time, across the intervening years, wondering why, and I no longer think the answer matters as much as I once believed it did. Some inheritances are not explained. They are simply carried, and eventually, if the person carrying them is patient and lucky enough, set down somewhere that can finally hold their full weight.*

He felt Meen-4's presence shift, from her corner in the laboratory, into something quieter than he had felt from her in some while — not surprise, still, Formicans did not experience surprise the way humans did. Something closer to witness.

*You have said something,* she sent, *that I did not know, in five years of watching you, you were carrying.*

*I've known since Arc IV,* he sent back. *I didn't know what to do with it until now. I'm not certain I fully understand it even now. I only understand that it needed saying, and that I finally have somewhere to say it that could actually hold it.*

He looked at the small, permanent pattern a while longer, the record of a dissolution that had, forty million years later, finally been given a resting place and a name spoken over it, and felt, for the first time in longer than he could easily account for, something in his own chest settle the same quiet, permanent way.

Dr. Shen Yue waited, patient, her figure still unshared, and he understood, when he finally turned his attention back toward her, that she had known exactly what he needed the silence for, and had simply, without being asked, given it to him.
