# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Sixty-Three — The Investigator's Circuit

Elder Kuo Tianming entered the 7-Eleven at 11:40 AM on the same Wednesday.

He bought nothing. He stood at the counter for four minutes speaking with Xiao Dong about the node monitoring equipment he was posing as a geological sensor technician checking. Xiao Dong, who had the adaptable social ease of someone who dealt with a range of unusual customers, helped him locate the building's fuse box access panel. The panel was near the drinks case, which put it approximately three meters from the northwest corner.

Kuo examined the fuse box for six minutes with equipment that was not designed for fuse boxes and that registered no Qi anomaly, because the threshold in the northwest corner was calibrated to pass through at Ratio 1.5 and Kuo's sensitivity in passive scan mode was not at the resolution required to detect something that precise.

He noted the corner as a weak signal area — consistent with the node depletion's geography — and updated his field report.

He wrote: *Node approaching terminal depletion. Source of depletion remains unidentified. No active practitioner signature in immediate vicinity.* A pause. Then: *The previous night clerk — named Lin Mushi, employed here from approximately 8 months before the depletion began, resigned approximately 3 weeks ago. Contact information: per employee records provided by manager. Investigating further.*

He left the store at 11:51 AM.

He was one degree of separation from Lin Mushi.

Lin Mushi was, at this moment, sixty meters north of the threshold in a vaulted hall thirty-two meters tall, watching the lit floor respond to his footsteps, writing observations in a notebook.

Kuo walked north along Wuhou Avenue, extending his perception in a scan that covered roughly forty meters in every direction.

He felt nothing unusual.

The threshold was designed for practitioners of Ratio 1.5 and above. Kuo operated at a standard Compression level that the Codex would have classified as approximately Ratio 1.2 — significant, skilled, but not fine enough for what was embedded in the wall two blocks behind him.

He recorded: *Area ambient Qi: consistent with depleted node characteristics. No practitioner activity detected.*

He moved on.

---

Lin Mushi, in the Strata, did not know the investigator had been in the store. He was sixty meters below the surface in a hall that was responding to his footsteps and watching, from the edge of his sixty-meter perception range, the distant signature of a living presence somewhere ahead.

He had been in the Strata for forty minutes when he made a discovery that superseded everything else he'd been thinking about.

The presence ahead was not moving toward him. It was not responding to him. But the ambient Qi field in the hall between him and the presence had a quality he recognized from the ant colony observation in the park — a field-level coherence that was not coming from either of them individually.

The Colony Link.

He could feel it, very faintly, in the space between himself and whatever was ahead of him. The distributed field of an active colony, running through the material of the hall's walls and floor and vault.

Not a human colony. The Qi signature was wrong for human metabolism — slower, denser, with a harmonic component he didn't have a baseline for.

He wrote in his notebook with a hand that was steadier than he expected:

> *2:37 AM. Strata, Layer 2, approximately 60 meters in from threshold.*
>
> *Living presence at estimated 80–100 meter distance, north-northwest. Not human. Metabolic signature: unknown.*
>
> *Colony Link field: active, distributed through hall material. The hall itself is part of the network.*
>
> *I am in a place that has occupants.*
>
> *Staying very still.*
>
> *The floor glows when I step on it.*

He looked at the floor.

It was still glowing where he stood.

He was a light source in this dark.

He stayed very still and thought about what to do next.
