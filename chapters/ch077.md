# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Seventy-Seven — What He Looks Like From Outside

Gao Yichen brought leftover noodles on a Sunday.

He had texted first: *made too many, dropping some off, don't argue*, which was Gao's method of expressing concern in a language that didn't require the concern to be named. Lin Mushi had not argued. He had been in the Strata for six hours the previous night and had come home and fallen asleep at 3 AM without eating and had woken at noon with the specific hunger of someone who had spent the night being more fully present in multiple dimensions simultaneously.

He texted back: *come in when you get here, door is open.*

Gao arrived at twelve-fifteen with a container of noodle soup and found Lin Mushi on the floor of the apartment in the lotus position, eyes open, very still.

Gao stopped in the doorway. He looked. He set the soup on the desk.

"You're doing a thing," he said.

"Compression practice," Lin Mushi said. "Ninety seconds." He came out of it smoothly, without the gasp that had accompanied the early attempts. "Thank you for the soup."

Gao sat in the desk chair and looked at him. "What does compression practice look like from outside?"

Lin Mushi thought about this. "What does it look like?"

"You flickered," Gao said.

He had been at Ratio 1.5, which was apparently sufficient to produce an observable visual distortion from a certain angle. He noted this. "A shimmer?"

"Like a bad TV signal. For maybe three seconds." Gao leaned forward. "Are you okay?"

"Yes. The shimmer is a side effect of the interface geometry being at a different scale than normal. At 1.5 it's mild." He got up from the floor and sat in the chair opposite. "At full Ratio 2 it's apparently more significant. I haven't tested that above 30 seconds."

"What happens at Ratio 2 for thirty seconds?"

"I haven't had anyone observe from outside. I should test that."

Gao looked at him for a moment. "You want me to watch."

"If you're willing."

Gao looked like a physicist being offered an unusual experimental opportunity, which was exactly what he was. "Yes," he said.

---

They ate the soup first. Lin Mushi ate properly and with attention, because he'd learned that Ratio 2 on an empty stomach was a bad idea, and Gao watched him eat with the expression of someone running a parallel assessment on nutritional status.

Then Lin Mushi sat on the floor again. He found the Compression geometry. He compressed.

"You're at 1.5?" Gao said.

"Yes. Pushing to 2 now."

The push: the geometry reducing further, the world's Qi density increasing, the protein channels in his hands becoming visible at the finer scale, the floor's ambient filament network resolving into detail he didn't have at Ratio 1.

"Oh," Gao said. Just that.

Lin Mushi came back to Ratio 1 at thirty seconds. "What did you see?"

Gao was quiet for a moment, the silence of someone organizing what they'd just seen into language. "At 1.5, flickering. At 2 — you were still there but you weren't... solid. Like a double exposure. The first layer was you normally. The second layer was you but slightly smaller, slightly brighter."

"The interface geometry," Lin Mushi said. "The primary and secondary presence."

"It resolved when you came back." Gao paused. "It was not alarming, exactly. But it was one of the stranger things I've seen."

"That tracks." Lin Mushi picked up his soup. "At Ratio 4, per the Codex, I'd be approximately the size of a grain of rice. If the scaling's linear, which I doubt."

"Rice." Gao repeated this with a specific expression. "Okay."

They sat with that for a moment.

"The forum post," Lin Mushi said. "ShenyueLab. I should reply."

Gao looked at him. "Yeah. You should reply." He picked up his tea. "You should have replied two months ago."

"I know." Lin Mushi thought about the seventh audio file. *The debt is specific. Owed to specific people.* "I'm going to."

He wrote the reply that evening, after Gao left.

It was not the whole explanation — he didn't have the whole explanation ready to share. But it was a beginning.

He sent it.

Then he turned back to the Strata and what waited in the library.

There was transmission to begin.
