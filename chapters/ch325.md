# FORMICA INFINITUM

## Chapter Three Hundred and Twenty-Five — What's Causing the Synchronization

The cross-referencing took three days, Liang Jingzhi's meticulous timeline data laid alongside Meen-4's careful records of the Witness Node's activity, the two of them, along with Lin Mushi, gradually assembling a picture neither dataset could have produced alone.

*The correlation is precise,* Meen-4 signaled, on the third evening, the three of them gathered in the administrative chamber around a shared display showing both timelines overlaid. *The Ring's synchronization began within eleven days of a measurable intensification in the Witness Node's inbound signal strength. This is not coincidental. The confidence interval is high enough that I am comfortable stating this as established fact rather than hypothesis.*

"So the two things are connected," Lin Mushi said.

*They appear to share a common cause,* Meen-4 clarified. *I would not yet describe the Ring's synchronization as directly caused by the Witness Node's signal. I would describe both as likely responses to the same external source — an intensifying attention, directed at this world, that is producing measurable physical effects through more than one channel.*

He sat with that, the specific unease of the phrase settling into his chest. "What kind of attention produces a physical effect like this? I thought monitoring meant observation. Passive. Not something that shows up as an actual force acting on structures forty million years old."

*I do not have a complete answer,* Meen-4 admitted. *But I can offer an informed hypothesis, based on older Formican records of comparable phenomena. Some forms of long-range observation, conducted across the distances the Collective operates at, require the projection of a sustained Veritonal current toward the observed location — not to interfere directly, but simply to maintain the connection necessary for detailed monitoring to function at all. If that hypothesis is correct, what you are detecting in both the Ring and the Witness Node may simply be the physical signature of the Collective's attention intensifying, rather than any deliberate action being taken against Earth specifically.*

"Simply," Chen Yufei repeated, having joined partway through the explanation, something dry entering her voice despite the gravity of the conversation. "You're describing something powerful enough to synchronize structures across an ocean floor as simply monitoring."

*I understand the word sounds inadequate given the scale of the effect,* Meen-4 signaled. *I use it deliberately, however, because the distinction matters. A monitoring current, however physically significant, is not the same as an attack, or even deliberate interference. I do not want either of you to conflate the two, because the appropriate response to each is considerably different.*

---

"What's the appropriate response to increased monitoring?" Lin Mushi asked.

*Continued preparation, not panic,* Meen-4 signaled. *And, I would suggest, continued transparency in how you conduct yourself, rather than any attempt at concealment. If the Collective's attention is genuinely intensifying because this world has become more active and more visible — through your own cultivation, through the Strata City, through Dr. Shen Yue's paper now entering scientific circulation — then the correct response is to continue building carefully and honestly, demonstrating exactly the kind of judgment that a watching body would want to see in a world it is deciding how to classify.*

Liang Jingzhi, who had been quiet through most of the explanation, finally spoke. "I'd like to keep monitoring the Ring closely regardless. If the synchronization spreads to the other nodes, or intensifies further, I want to know immediately rather than discovering it during my next scheduled survey."

"Agreed," Lin Mushi said. "Daily monitoring, at minimum, until we understand the trend better."

*I will do the same with the Witness Node,* Meen-4 signaled. *If the signal strength continues intensifying at its current rate, I would estimate several months before it reaches a threshold significant enough to warrant more urgent concern. That estimate carries considerable uncertainty. I offer it as a working timeline, not a guarantee.*

---

He walked with Chen Yufei back toward the residential wing afterward, the evening's careful, methodical investigation sitting heavily in his chest despite Meen-4's insistence that panic wasn't warranted.

"Are you scared?" Chen Yufei asked, her hand finding his in the quiet corridor.

He considered the question honestly, rather than reaching for reassurance. "Some. Not overwhelmed by it. I think — I think this is the first time the Collective's attention has felt like something with actual physical weight, rather than an abstract fact Meen-4 told us about weeks ago. Knowing something intellectually and feeling its effect on structures forty million years old are different kinds of knowing."

"What do we do with that?"

"The same thing we've done with everything else this world has handed us," he said. "Watch it carefully. Prepare for what we can prepare for. And keep building the things that actually matter, because I don't think stopping, or hiding, or pretending this isn't happening does anything except waste the time we still have to prepare properly."

She squeezed his hand, and they walked the rest of the way in a silence that carried, beneath its quiet, the specific steady resolve of two people choosing, deliberately, to keep moving forward into something neither of them yet fully understood.
