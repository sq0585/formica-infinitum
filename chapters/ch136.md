# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Thirty-Six — Axis-7's Second Lesson

She came to him two days after the document release.

He was working in the archive on the translation document's follow-up materials — detailed notation analyses for the recipients who had asked technical questions — when she walked directly to the table and sat down. Not her usual east-wall position. The table.

He looked up.

She sent the register signal without preamble: *You will reach the Intermediate Archive's restricted section within four months.*

He had not told her his progress timeline. He had been working with Meen-4's exchange arrangement — annotated translations for deeper access — but hadn't shared his estimates of how far along the access progression he was.

"How do you know that?" he said.

*I have been observing your comprehension development for four months.* A pause. *Your access curve accelerates at the correct rate. Four months is approximate.*

He noted this without commenting on it. The observation that she'd been tracking his development specifically.

*The restricted section contains the Dimensional Weaver curriculum,* she continued. *This is the material that defines the next cultivation stage. When you reach it, you should be able to open it correctly rather than incorrectly.*

He thought about the dimensional lock on the Scale Walking manual — the dual-scale circuit he'd built to open it, the parallel operation at two Compression ratios simultaneously. The restricted section's lock would be more sophisticated.

*What's the difference between correctly and incorrectly?*

*Incorrectly: forcing the lock through raw Compression capability or through Chitin Circuit brute-force. The lock opens but the mechanism is stressed. The material inside can be accessed but some of the more sensitive calibration content will be offset — you'll read it as slightly different from what it actually says. Minor errors will compound.*

*Correctly: using the Dimensional Lock navigation technique, which maintains both the lock's integrity and your own interface integrity during the crossing.* She paused. *The technique I'm about to show you.*

He set his pen down.

She showed him.

---

The Dimensional Lock navigation technique was not primarily a Compression exercise. It was a perception exercise — specifically, a method for perceiving a multi-dimensional lock's architecture simultaneously across the lock's different spatial dimensions, without collapsing the observation into a single-scale view that missed the interactions between dimensions.

It required Colony Link sensitivity extended to the lock itself: reading the lock as a system with emergent properties, not as a collection of individual mechanisms.

He spent four hours learning the basics. The technique required a quality of attention he hadn't applied to object-level interaction before — the same distributed awareness he'd been developing for human group dynamics, now applied to an engineered mechanism.

At the end of the session, he could perceive the restricted section's lock architecture at two of its three dimensions simultaneously.

Axis-7 said: *Adequate for the current stage. The third dimension requires practice.* She stood. *Practice on the secondary locks. The mechanism is similar, the calibration is lower stakes.*

She moved to leave.

He said: *Why are you helping me?*

She stopped.

She turned back.

The signal she sent was the longest he'd received from her, and the one he had to work hardest to parse. He got eighty-five percent.

The substance: *The Inheritance Protocol's purpose is the continuation of Formican knowledge through Carrier integration. My assignment is to facilitate Carrier access and development. Providing technical assistance to a Carrier who is developing capability is within the scope of my assignment.*

He waited. Because there was more.

*Additionally,* she continued, *the Carriers I have observed — through records, not personally — maintained isolation from surface culture as a primary operating mode. You have not. The document you released: this is not an isolated Carrier's approach. The consequences will be different. Whether different means better or worse depends on what you do next.* A pause. *I want you to do next things well. Therefore I am ensuring you have the correct technique for the next things.*

He looked at her.

He sent back: *You have a stake in what I do.*

*I have been here for one hundred and eighty years,* she said. *Yes.*

He thought about that.

*I will practice the secondary locks,* he said. *Thank you.*

She went back to the east wall.

He went back to his translation work.

He had four months before the restricted section.

He began practicing the Dimensional Lock navigation immediately, on the secondary locks he'd already opened once and could now approach as training grounds rather than sealed systems.

He noted the date in his notebook and drew an arrow forward.

*Four months.*

Plenty of time to practice, and to do what came between now and then.
