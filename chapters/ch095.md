# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter Ninety-Five — The Strata Entry Point

Lady Shen Baoling arrived in Chengdu on a Thursday.

Lin Mushi knew this not because he had been monitoring arrivals — that was beyond his current reach — but because the city's ambient Qi field changed on Thursday afternoon in a way that he recognized from Elder Kuo's arrival two months ago: a new structured presence, more controlled than the coffee-buying practitioner, more powerful than Elder Kuo, moving through Wuhou District with the deliberate attention of a person who had been given coordinates and was following them.

He had been expecting this.

What he had not been expecting was the presence's range.

Elder Kuo's ambient scan radius had been approximately forty meters. Lady Shen's was at least eighty. He could feel her from his apartment window, two streets over, without any active effort on his part. She wasn't scanning — she was simply *present*, and her presence projected.

He activated the directional suppression and watched her move through the ambient field without, apparently, locating him. She continued north. Her pattern suggested she was mapping the area rather than searching for a specific point — she had coordinates, probably his address from the investigation report, but she was doing a preliminary survey before going directly there.

She was careful.

He wrote:

> *Lady Shen Baoling: confirmed in Chengdu as of Thursday PM. Ambient projection range: 80+ meters. Distinguishing characteristic from Elder Kuo: she is not scanning. She is simply present at a level that covers 80 meters. The difference matters — scanning is active effort, projection is passive state. She is operating without effort at a level Elder Kuo had to work to achieve.*
>
> *She did not locate me in my apartment. Directional suppression effective at this range.*
>
> *Her survey pattern: systematic, north-south transects across the target area. She is building a picture before approaching.*
>
> *This is not an acquisition in progress. This is reconnaissance.*
>
> *She will approach when she has the picture she wants.*

He put the suppression down after she moved out of range.

Then he went back to the question he'd been working on when she arrived: the thin section.

---

He had located the thin section six weeks ago — the spot where the geological boundary between surface and Strata was forty meters rather than the standard two hundred, directly below a parking structure a few blocks north of the store. He'd been using it as his entry point for the past eleven visits.

What the Chitin Circuit had revealed, in the three days since he'd deployed a unit near the thin section, was something he hadn't been able to perceive directly: the thin section was thinning.

Not dramatically. A few centimeters per week, at the circuit's measurement resolution. But the trend was clear: the boundary layer was compressing slowly toward the Strata's surface.

He had two hypotheses. First: the thin section was a natural geological feature that shifted with tectonic movement, and the compression was coincidental. Second: the thin section was not natural — it was a deliberately maintained entry point, and its current state of compression was a response to his use of it.

The Strata was adjusting to being accessed.

If hypothesis two was correct, the thin section's compression would continue until the boundary layer was thin enough to be crossed at Ratio 1.3 rather than 1.5 — lowering the entry barrier. Making it easier to enter.

The Strata was making it easier to enter.

He wrote:

> *Thin section status: 40m boundary, decreasing at ~2cm/week. If trend continues: 38m in one week, 35m in two weeks. At 30m, entry Compression requirement drops below 1.5.*
>
> *Hypothesis 2 (active adjustment): consistent with the Strata's recognition behavior — the floor illumination, the Keeper communication, the library door's access criteria. The Strata has systems that respond to Chitin Seed presence.*
>
> *If the entry point is being adjusted to accommodate me: the Strata is facilitating my access. This implies a position. Not ownership — facilitation. The Strata wants to be accessible to me.*
>
> *Implication for Lady Shen: she cannot use this entry point without meeting the compression requirement. At her current apparent level — I'm estimating Ratio 1.2–1.3 based on her ambient projection — she's at or below threshold.*
>
> *The Strata's access control is more effective than any security measure I could build.*

He looked at the thin section data on his phone.

Lady Shen was in the city. She would find him eventually — his address was in the investigation file and directional suppression was not indefinite. He had perhaps a few days before she made direct contact.

He needed to be in a position of strength when that happened. Not physical strength — he was not under any illusion about a direct confrontation with a practitioner of her level. Diplomatic strength. Institutional context. The kind of position that made acquisition complicated rather than straightforward.

He had the Iron Meridian introduction being arranged.

He had the Keeper communication ongoing.

He had the Strata's access protocol effectively filtering who could enter.

And he had, he noted, one more thing.

He called Gao.

"I need you to know where I am at all times for the next week," he said.

A pause. "Okay. Why?"

"Someone is in the city who may try to move me somewhere I don't want to go. If I stop responding to messages for more than twelve hours, contact Chen Yufei."

Another pause. "Is this a 'you're in danger' situation or a 'you have a plan' situation?"

"Both," Lin Mushi said. "But the plan is good."

"Okay," Gao said. "I'm assuming the plan involves something underground."

"Yes."

"Of course it does." A pause. "Send me a location check every morning."

"I will."

He sent the first one immediately.

Then he put the phone down and went back to Document 6.

The Keeper's signal was becoming clearer.

He was going to need it.
