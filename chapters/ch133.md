# FORMICA INFINITUM
## Chapter One Hundred and Thirty-Three — The Document Goes Out

He sent it at 6 AM and went to bed.

He slept until noon, which was either wisdom or avoidance and he had decided not to examine the distinction closely. He ate lunch. He watered the plant on the windowsill, which had been steadily recovering since the directed release experiment four months ago and was now genuinely thriving, which remained quietly remarkable every time he noticed it.

Then he opened his phone.

Twelve replies in six hours.

He read them in the order they arrived.

---

The first: a senior Iron Meridian practitioner in Beijing named Wei Jianming, whom Chen Yufei had described as someone who had been asking uncomfortable questions about the foundational texts for twenty years. He wrote: *Our foundational text says something different from the Northern Peak's foundational text. Yours says that our foundational text and theirs say the same thing, in different language, derived from the same source. How is that possible? Please explain the derivation in more detail.*

Not hostile. Not defensive. A precise request for more information.

The second: a Celestial Compass School astronomer whom Chen Yufei's contact had described as someone who had spent fifteen years trying to explain Qi flow patterns using conventional astronomy and had been troubled by the gaps. She wrote: *The notation in your document — it is not the same as the fragments we have preserved in our astronomical records, but it is related. Can you share more of the source notation? I would like to compare it to our records systematically.*

The third: a Verdant Stream Collective elder from Yunnan, brief: *This explains what I have been observing in the forest Qi flows for thirty years. I would like to talk.*

The fourth through eighth: acknowledgments, questions, requests for more material. Some personal, some formal. All engaged with the content.

The ninth: from the Celestial Compass School contact in Leshan — the one who had told Chen Yufei about the propagation anomaly. She wrote: *I was at a cultivation gathering in Leshan two days ago. Twelve of your recipients were there. We discussed your document before it arrived (we knew it was coming) and after. The discussion was — significant. Several people have questions they'd like to ask you directly. Is that possible?*

Twelve people. In the same room before the document arrived. Having the conversation in real-time, with the Veritonal propagation anomaly already a context, and then receiving the document the following morning.

He had timed the release for exactly this.

The twelfth reply: from Lady Shen Baoling, separate from the group, sent at 9:43 AM.

*I have read this twice. The Northern Peak Court's foundational text describes the same process as the Formican Standard Protocol's Phase Four through Seven, in different notation, with significant efficiency losses in the transmission. This is accurate. I have known that our technique was less efficient than the theoretical maximum for twenty years. This explains why.*

*I have three questions. The first two I can answer myself by continuing to read the archive material. The third: what do you think the Courts should do with this information?*

He held that last question.

He thought about it for an hour.

Then he replied:

*I think the Courts should decide for themselves. That's the honest answer. I can tell you what the information is and what it accurately describes. What institutions do in response to accurate information about their own foundations is their work, not mine.*

*But for what it's worth: I think the practitioners who have spent their lives on these techniques deserve to know why they work. Not as a rebuke. As a completion of what they were doing. The techniques are real. The practitioners were right to practice them. They just weren't told the whole story.*

He sent this.

He opened his notebook.

He wrote:

> *Document released, 6 AM. 12 replies in 6 hours. Initial reads: engagement with content rather than defensive rejection. The recipient selection worked.*
>
> *Lady Shen's third question: what should the Courts do? I don't have a mandate here. I have information and a translation. What the institutions build from it is not mine to specify.*
>
> *What I can do: be available to answer questions, continue sharing accurate information, continue developing my own capability and understanding so that what I share is more complete over time.*
>
> *What comes next: more replies. Some harder than these. Some from people who have more invested in the existing structure. The selection tried to target the honest and the troubled — but honest and troubled people exist within institutions that will respond to this disruption in their own way.*
>
> *The disruption has been started.*

He capped his pen.

The city outside was doing its Tuesday business — deliveries, traffic, the noodle shop below beginning its lunch service. He could hear these things with his ordinary senses. He could feel, at very low resolution, the ambient Qi field of the street, the steady hum of the Under-Web's repaired tributaries, the distant warmth of the Formican community's collective field below.

He had sent the document.

Everything that came next would come from that.

He was ready for it.

He was not, he noted, anything other than calm.

That was, he thought, what understanding felt like when it was sufficient.
