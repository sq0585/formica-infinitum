> *"The world you live in is a surface. Beneath it is everything that has ever mattered."*

---

**Formica Infinitum** is a web serial following Lin Mushi — a 19-year-old university dropout working the night shift at a Chengdu convenience store — who, at 3:17 AM on a Tuesday in October, watches an old man die at his counter and inherits four hundred years of hidden knowledge in 0.4 seconds.

What follows is not a power fantasy. It is the story of a very curious person, a very old map, and a world with far more layers than anyone on the surface has been told about.

The story begins small: a warm phone, a notebook, a depth beneath the floor that shouldn't be there. It ends at the scale of the universe. It takes its time getting there.

---

**Genre:** Cultivation fantasy · Urban mystery · Hard science fiction  
**Setting:** Chengdu, China — and what lies beneath it  
**Status:** Complete — 542 chapters across 7 arcs
